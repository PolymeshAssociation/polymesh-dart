# Sender/Receiver Affirmations

## Affirmations
Once has settlement has been posted on chain, sender/receiver send their agreement along with a proof which proves that they are the party to that leg, and they have done a correct account state transition, invalidating previous state by submitting the nullifier and their revealing their new state. Note that they don't point to their old state in the accumulator.

For an account state to transition from $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}}$ to $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}$,
- secret key $\mathrm{sk}$, identity $\mathrm{id}$ and asset-id $\mathrm{at}$ should not change
- nullifier should be revealed as $\rho^i \cdot G_5$
- nullifier secret key $\rho^i$ and randomness $s^i$ should change as: $\rho^{i+1} = \rho \cdot \rho^i$, $s^{i+1} = s \cdot s^i$

Old state
$$
\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{balance} \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{counter} \cdot G_2 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + \rho^i \cdot G_5 + s \cdot G_6 + s^i \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8 + \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \quad \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}} \in \mathbb{G}_p
$$

New state
$$
\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{balance}' \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{counter}' \cdot G_2 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + \rho^{i+1} \cdot G_5 + s \cdot G_6 + s^{i+1} \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8 + \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \quad \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}} \in \mathbb{G}_p
$$

$\mathrm{balance}$ might or might not be same as $\mathrm{balance}'$ depending on who is affirming or what kind of transaction it is. $\mathrm{counter}'$ might be same or 1 more or 1 less than $\mathrm{counter}$ depending on the kind of transaction.
Also, it must be proved that the $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}}$ has same asset-id $\mathrm{at}$ that is in the leg and has the public key for the same secret key as in the account.


For a single leg settlement of amount $v$:

1. When a sender affirms a leg, its new account state's balance should be $v$ less than the old account's and its counter should increase by 1.
2. When a receiver affirms a leg, its new account state's balance should be same as the old account's and its counter should increase by 1.
3. Sender can revert its affirmation by creating a new account state which has the balance increased by $v$ and counter decreased by 1.
4. Receiver can revert its affirmation by creating a new account state with counter decreased by 1.
5. Once a settlement has been confirmed, i.e. it cant be reverted,
    - sender can send a counter update txn to decrease it's counter by 1
    - receiver can send a claim funds txn to increases it's balance by $v$ and decrease it's counter by 1.

However, sender and receiver do have an option to send an irreversible affirmation and in that case, they don't change their counter, unless the settlement expires in which case the party who had sent, reverses it. 

Table describing changes to balance and counter for various transactions. Subscripts $_s$ and $_r$ refer to txn sent by sender and receiver respectively.

| Transaction Type     | Description                                       | Balance Change | Counter Change |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | -------------- | -------------- |
| **$\mathrm{Affirm}_s$**       | Sender affirms a leg of amount $v$                | **-v**         | **+1**         |
| **$\mathrm{Affirm}_r$**       | Receiver affirms a leg                            | **0**          | **+1**         |
| **$\mathrm{Claim}_r$**        | Receiver claims funds after settlement            | **+v**         | **-1**         |
| **$\mathrm{CntUpd}_s$**       | Sender updates counter after settlement           | **0**          | **-1**         |
| **$\mathrm{Reverse}_s$**      | Sender reverses their affirmation                 | **+v**         | **-1**         |
| **$\mathrm{Reverse}_r$**      | Receiver reverses their affirmation               | **0**          | **-1**         |
| **$\mathrm{Irr}$-$\mathrm{Affirm}_s$** | Sender irreversibly affirms a leg of amount $v$   | **-v**         | **0**          |
| **$\mathrm{Irr}$-$\mathrm{Affirm}_r$** | Receiver irreversibly affirms a leg of amount $v$ | **+v**         | **0**          |

### Leg configuration

A leg is created with two configuration options (see `LegEncConfig` and the "Leg" section in `4.md`):

- `reveal_asset_id`: if true, asset-id $\mathrm{at}$ is revealed publicly to the verifier and there is no $\mathrm{CT}_{\mathrm{at}}$ ciphertext (the $r_4$ component of all ephemeral keys is also absent); if false, $\mathrm{at}$ is encrypted as $\mathrm{CT}_{\mathrm{at}} = r_4 \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + \mathrm{at} \cdot H$.
- `visibility`: a `PartyVisibility` enum over which party can decrypt the counterparty's encryption public key — `NoVisibility`, `FullVisibility`, `OnlySenderSeesReceiver`, `OnlyReceiverSeesSender`. When the sender may see the receiver, the sender's ephemeral key carries an extra component for decrypting the receiver's encryption public key, and symmetrically for the receiver. **This option does not affect the affirmation proof** — each affirmer only ever uses their own ephemeral keys; the cross-keys only matter for decryption.

When an affirmation covers multiple legs of the same account (e.g. a swap settlement where the prover is sender of one leg and receiver of another), the asset-id might be revealed in some legs but not others. So per leg the affirmation proof distinguishes three sub-cases:

1. **Asset-id hidden** — no leg in this affirmation reveals $\mathrm{at}$.
2. **Asset-id revealed in this leg** — $\mathrm{at}$ is part of this leg's public data.
3. **Asset-id revealed elsewhere** — this leg has $\mathrm{CT}_{\mathrm{at}}$, but some other leg of the same affirmation reveals $\mathrm{at}$, so $\mathrm{at}$ is public to the verifier.

### Protocol

_The following description is for W1 (solo) where a single prover knows all witnesses including the secret keys $\mathrm{sk}$ and $\mathrm{ek}$. The split-prover variant used in W2/W3 is described after the Verifier section below._

This section describes a unified account state transition proof which can handle all the above scenarios and parts of this proof can be omitted when balance doesn't change.

Here the prover (sender/receiver) wants to prove following relations when sending affirmation for leg with amount $v$:

1. $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{bal}_0 \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{cnt}_0 \cdot G_2 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + \rho^i \cdot G_5 + s \cdot G_6 + s^i \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8 + \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$
2. Similarly, $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{cnt}_1 \cdot G_2 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + \rho^{i+1} \cdot G_5 + s \cdot G_6 + s^{i+1} \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8 + \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$
3. $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}}$ exists in the accumulator (account curve tree)
4. $N = \rho^i \cdot G_5$
5. $\rho^{i+1} = \rho \cdot \rho^i$
6. $s^{i+1} = s \cdot s^i$
7. If balance change is expected,
    - $\mathrm{bal}_1 = \mathrm{bal}_0 - v$ (for **Affirm_s**, **$\mathrm{Irr}$-$\mathrm{Affirm}_s$**)
    - $\mathrm{bal}_1 = \mathrm{bal}_0 + v$ (for **Claim_r**, **Reverse_s**, **$\mathrm{Irr}$-$\mathrm{Affirm}_r$**)
8. Counter change:
    - $\mathrm{cnt}_1 = \mathrm{cnt}_0 + 1$ (**Affirm_s**, **Affirm_r**)
    - $\mathrm{cnt}_1 = \mathrm{cnt}_0 - 1$ (**Claim_r**, **CntUpd_s**, **Reverse_s**, **Reverse_r**)
    - $\mathrm{cnt}_1 = \mathrm{cnt}_0$ (**$\mathrm{Irr}$-$\mathrm{Affirm}_s$**, **$\mathrm{Irr}$-$\mathrm{Affirm}_r$**)
9. Since the change to counter is public, relation for $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}$ can be expressed as:
    - $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}} - G_2 = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{cnt} \cdot G_2 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + \rho^{i+1} \cdot G_5 + s \cdot G_6 + s^{i+1} \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8 + \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$ if counter increased by 1.
    - $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}} + G_2 = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{cnt} \cdot G_2 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + \rho^{i+1} \cdot G_5 + s \cdot G_6 + s^{i+1} \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8 + \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$ if counter decreased by 1.
    - $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{cnt} \cdot G_2 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + \rho^{i+1} \cdot G_5 + s \cdot G_6 + s^{i+1} \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8 + \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$ if counter remains same.
10. $\mathrm{bal}_1 \leq \mathrm{MAX\_BALANCE}$ to avoid overflows
11. Prove that the party's account corresponds to the party in the leg, and bind the leg's private plaintexts (the amount, and the asset-id when it is encrypted) to the account. The account commitment contains $\mathrm{ek}$ on $G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$. The Bulletproof commitment includes both $\mathrm{ek}$ and $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ with the constraint $\mathrm{ek} \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1} = 1$, which allows $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ to be used in ciphertext proofs. There is **no** separate participant ciphertext proof; instead each leg is tied to the account through its amount ciphertext $\mathrm{CT}_v$ and/or its asset-id ciphertext $\mathrm{CT}_{\mathrm{at}}$. Every leg requires at least one of these, and each uses $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ — bound to the account by $\mathrm{ek} \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1} = 1$ — as the witness that proves the account holds the leg's key. (These correspond to the `AmountOnly`, `AssetIdOnly`, and `AssetIdAndAmount` variants of `LegAccountLink`.) Per leg:
    - **(11a) Amount ciphertext — proved when the asset-id is revealed in this leg, or when the balance changes** (i.e. $\mathrm{needs\_ct\_amount} = \mathrm{revealed\_in\_this\_leg} \lor \mathrm{balance\_changes}$). Prove knowledge of $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ and $v$ in $\mathrm{CT}_v = \mathrm{Eph}_v \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1} + H \cdot v$, where $\mathrm{Eph}_v$ is the party's ephemeral key for the amount ciphertext. $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ comes from the Bulletproof commitment; $v$ is internal to this proof. When the balance changes, the $v$ response is additionally shared into the Bulletproof balance commitment (step 9), so the balance change equals the leg's encrypted amount. When the asset-id is revealed in this leg and the balance does **not** change (e.g. $\mathrm{Affirm}_r$, $\mathrm{CntUpd}_s$, $\mathrm{Reverse}_r$ on a leg whose asset-id is revealed in that leg), this amount proof is the **only** tie between the account and the leg, which is why $\mathrm{CT}_v$ is proved even though no balance changes.
    - **(11b) Asset-id ciphertext — proved when the asset-id is encrypted in this leg** (i.e. $\mathrm{needs\_ct\_asset\_id} = \lnot\,\mathrm{revealed\_in\_this\_leg}$), in one of two forms:
        - **Asset-id hidden** (no leg in this affirmation reveals $\mathrm{at}$): prove knowledge of $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ and $\mathrm{at}$ in $\mathrm{CT}_{\mathrm{at}} = \mathrm{Eph}_{\mathrm{at}} \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1} + H \cdot \mathrm{at}$, where $\mathrm{Eph}_{\mathrm{at}}$ is the party's ephemeral key for the asset-id ciphertext. $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ comes from the Bulletproof commitment; $\mathrm{at}$ is shared from the account state commitment. This binds the leg's encrypted asset-id to the account's asset-id.
        - **Asset-id revealed but not in this leg** (some other leg of this affirmation reveals $\mathrm{at}$, so $\mathrm{at}$ is public): prove knowledge of $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ in $\mathrm{CT}_{\mathrm{at}} - H \cdot \mathrm{at} = \mathrm{Eph}_{\mathrm{at}} \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1}$. $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ comes from the Bulletproof commitment.
    - When the asset-id is revealed in this leg, $\mathrm{at}$ is public and is handled by subtracting $\mathrm{at} \cdot G_3$ from the state targets (see steps 6–7); such a leg has no asset-id ciphertext and so carries only (11a).

    In short: a leg that reveals its asset-id carries only (11a); a leg that encrypts its asset-id carries (11b), and additionally (11a) when its balance changes.

**Instance**: $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}$, $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}$, $N$, $\mathrm{Path}_r$, $\mathrm{Root}$, $\mathrm{CT}_s$, $\mathrm{CT}_r$, $\mathrm{CT}_v$, $\mathrm{CT}_{\mathrm{at}}$ (a ciphertext when asset-id is hidden, or the revealed value $\mathrm{at}$ otherwise), $\mathrm{Eph}_s$, $\mathrm{Eph}_r$, $\mathrm{Eph}_v$, $\mathrm{Eph}_{\mathrm{at}}$ (only when asset-id is hidden), $\mathrm{EK}_s$, $\mathrm{EK}_r$, $G_{\mathrm{Aff}}$, $G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$, $G_i$, $H$, $H_i$, $\widetilde{G}$, $\widetilde{G}_i$, $\widetilde{H}$, $\widetilde{H}_i$

Here $G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$ is the generator for the $\mathrm{ek}$ component of the account commitment, and also serves as the key generator in ciphertexts. $H$ is the generator for plaintext values in ciphertexts.

**Witness**: $\mathrm{sk}, \mathrm{ek}, \mathrm{id}, v, \mathrm{bal}_0, \mathrm{bal}_1, \mathrm{cnt}, \rho, \rho^i, \rho^{i+1}, s, s^i, s^{i+1}, \mathrm{Path}$ and additionally $\mathrm{at}$ when asset-id is hidden. $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ is derived from $\mathrm{ek}$ and committed in the Bulletproof with constraint $\mathrm{ek} \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1} = 1$.

Above are the high level instance and witness values known before the protocol starts and don't include instance and witness created during protocol execution like the various blindings and $T$ values.

#### Prover
1. Prover first wants to prove knowledge of $\mathrm{sk}, \mathrm{ek}, \mathrm{at}, \mathrm{bal}_0, \mathrm{cnt}, \rho, \rho^i, s, s^i, \mathrm{id}$ in the relation $$\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{bal}_0 \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{cnt}_0 \cdot G_2 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + \rho^i \cdot G_5 + s \cdot G_6 + s^i \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8 + \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$$
2. Similarly, prover wants to prove knowledge of $\mathrm{sk}, \mathrm{ek}, \mathrm{at}, \mathrm{bal}_1, \mathrm{cnt}, \rho, \rho^{i+1}, s, s^{i+1}, \mathrm{id}$ in $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}$ from relation 9.
    - If counter increased by 1: $$\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}} - G_2 = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{cnt}_0 \cdot G_2 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + \rho^{i+1} \cdot G_5 + s \cdot G_6 + s^{i+1} \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8 + \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$$
    - If counter decreased by 1: $$\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}} + G_2 = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{cnt}_0 \cdot G_2 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + \rho^{i+1} \cdot G_5 + s \cdot G_6 + s^{i+1} \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8 + \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$$
    - If counter remains same: $$\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{cnt}_0 \cdot G_2 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + \rho^{i+1} \cdot G_5 + s \cdot G_6 + s^{i+1} \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8 + \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$$
3. Prover gets the path of the leaf $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}}$ as $\mathrm{Path}$, an array of nodes (curve points), and randomizes it by adding blindings to all nodes. This will result in the leaf $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}}$ being transformed into $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r} = \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}} + b_0 \cdot H_0$ and $\mathrm{Path}$ transforms into $\mathrm{Path}_r$. Prover enforces the constraints for curve tree membership using $\mathrm{Path}, \mathrm{Path}_r, b_i$.
4. Since prover knows the opening of $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}}$ and $b_0$, it can prove the knowledge of opening of $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}$ as well. So relation 1 can be transformed as:
   $$
 \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{bal}_0 \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{cnt}_0 \cdot G_2 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + \rho^i \cdot G_5 + s \cdot G_6 + s^i \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8 + \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + b_0 \cdot H_0 = \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}
 $$
5. Prover picks random $$\mathrm{sk}_r, \mathrm{ek}_r, \mathrm{at}_r, \mathrm{bal}_{0_r}, \mathrm{cnt}_r, \rho_r, \rho^i_r, \rho^{i+1}_r, s_r, s^i_r, s^{i+1}_r, \mathrm{id}_r, b_{0_r} \in \mathbb{Z}_p$$ and creates:
   $$T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}} = \mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{bal}_{0_r} \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{cnt}_r \cdot G_2 + \mathrm{at}_r \cdot G_3 + \rho_r \cdot G_4 + \rho^i_r \cdot G_5 + $$
   $$s_r \cdot G_6 + s^i_r \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{id}_r \cdot G_8 + \mathrm{ek}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + b_{0_r} \cdot H_0, \in \mathbb{G}_p$$
6. For the new state, create:
   $$T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}} = \mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{bal}_{1_r} \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{cnt}_r \cdot G_2 + \mathrm{at}_r \cdot G_3 + \rho_r \cdot G_4 + \rho^{i+1}_r \cdot G_5 + $$
   $$s_r \cdot G_6 + s^{i+1}_r \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{id}_r \cdot G_8 + \mathrm{ek}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}, \in \mathbb{G}_p$$

   Same blindings for $\mathrm{sk}, \mathrm{ek}, \mathrm{at}, \mathrm{cnt}, \rho, s, \mathrm{id}$ are used in both old and new state since these values don't change. If balance doesn't change, also use same blinding so $\mathrm{bal}_{0_r} = \mathrm{bal}_{1_r}$.
7. For the correctness of nullifier, prover creates:
   $$
 T_{\mathrm{null}} = \rho^i_r \cdot G_5, \in \mathbb{G}_p
 $$
8. For enforcing $\rho^{i+1} = \rho \cdot \rho^i$, $s^{i+1} = s \cdot s^i$ and $\mathrm{ek} \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1} = 1$, prover sets up the constraints in Bulletproof. The first two are multiplications for randomness evolution, the third links $\mathrm{ek}$ (committed in the account state) with $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ (needed for ciphertext proofs). Prover uses Sigma protocol (Chaum Pedersen) to prove equality of committed values in Bulletproof's commitment and in the state commitments. Let $C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\rho,s}}$ be the commitment:
     $$C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\rho,s}} = b' \cdot H_0 + \rho \cdot H_1 + \rho^i \cdot H_2 + \rho^{i+1} \cdot H_3 + s \cdot H_4 + s^i \cdot H_5 + s^{i+1} \cdot H_6 + \mathrm{ek} \cdot H_7 + \mathrm{ek}^{-1} \cdot H_8, \in \mathbb{G}_p$$
     _In W2/W3 the $\mathrm{ek} \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1} = 1$ constraint is not enforced via Bulletproofs; instead the device proves it via a Sigma protocol on a fresh element $D$, and $\mathrm{ek}, \mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ are dropped from $C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\rho,s}}$. See the split prover variant below._
9. If the balance changes, prover sets up constraint in Bulletproof based on transaction type:
    - If balance change is $-v$: $\mathrm{bal}_1 = \mathrm{bal}_0 - v$
    - If balance change is $+v$: $\mathrm{bal}_1 = \mathrm{bal}_0 + v$

   Let $C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}}$ be the commitment:
   $$
 C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}} = b'' \cdot H_0 + v \cdot H_1 + \mathrm{bal}_0 \cdot H_2 + \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot H_3, \in \mathbb{G}_p
 $$
10. Prover creates commitment for $C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\rho,s}}$ by picking random $r_{\mathrm{BP}_{\rho,s}}, \mathrm{ek}^{-1}_r \in \mathbb{Z}_p$:
    $$
 T_{\mathrm{BP}_{\rho,s}} = r_{\mathrm{BP}_{\rho,s}} \cdot H_0 + \rho_r \cdot H_1 + \rho^i_r \cdot H_2 + \rho^{i+1}_r \cdot H_3 + s_r \cdot H_4 + s^i_r \cdot H_5 + s^{i+1}_r \cdot H_6 + \mathrm{ek}_r \cdot H_7 + \mathrm{ek}^{-1}_r \cdot H_8, \in \mathbb{G}_p
 $$
11. Prover creates commitment for $C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}}$ by picking random $v_r, r_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}} \in \mathbb{Z}_p$:
    $$
 T_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}} = r_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}} \cdot H_0 + v_r \cdot H_1 + \mathrm{bal}_{0_r} \cdot H_2 + \mathrm{bal}_{1_r} \cdot H_3, \in \mathbb{G}_p
 $$
12. For the leg-account link, prover computes the $T$-values using shared blindings.
    - **Amount (when the asset-id is revealed in this leg, or the balance changes)**: $T_v = \mathrm{ek}^{-1}_r \cdot \mathrm{Eph}_v + v_r \cdot H$, using $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}_r$ from step 10 (Bulletproof commitment) and the amount blinding $v_r$. When the balance changes, $v_r$ is the same blinding used for $v$ in the balance Bulletproof commitment $T_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}}$ (step 11); when the asset-id is revealed in this leg and the balance does not change, $v_r$ is a fresh blinding internal to this proof.
    - **Asset-id (when the asset-id is encrypted in this leg)**:
        - **Asset-id hidden**: $T_{\mathrm{at}} = \mathrm{ek}^{-1}_r \cdot \mathrm{Eph}_{\mathrm{at}} + \mathrm{at}_r \cdot H$, using $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}_r$ from step 10 and $\mathrm{at}_r$ from step 5.
        - **Asset-id revealed but not in this leg**: $T_{\mathrm{at}} = \mathrm{ek}^{-1}_r \cdot \mathrm{Eph}_{\mathrm{at}}$, using $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}_r$ from step 10 ($\mathrm{at}$ is public, no blinding needed).
        - **Asset-id revealed in this leg**: no asset-id $T$-value.
15. Prover hashes the following to create challenge $c$ as:
    $$c = \mathrm{Hash}(\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}, \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}, N, \mathrm{Path}_r, \mathrm{Root}, \mathrm{txn\_type}, \mathrm{CT}_s, \mathrm{CT}_r, \mathrm{CT}_v, \mathrm{CT}_{\mathrm{at}}, \mathrm{Eph}_s (\text{or } \mathrm{Eph}_r), \mathrm{Eph}_{\mathrm{at}} (\text{or } \mathrm{at} \text{ if revealed}), \mathrm{Eph}_v, \mathrm{EK}_s (\text{or } \mathrm{EK}_r), C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\rho,s}}, C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}}, $$
    $$T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}, T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}}, T_{\mathrm{null}}, T_{\mathrm{BP}_{\rho,s}}, T_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}}, T_{\mathrm{at}}, T_v)$$
    ($T_v$ is present when the asset-id is revealed in this leg or the balance changes; $T_{\mathrm{at}}$ only when the asset-id is encrypted in this leg. The full leg encryption — including $\mathrm{CT}_s, \mathrm{CT}_r$ — is still hashed so the proof is bound to the specific leg, even though no participant relation is proved over $\mathrm{CT}_s/\mathrm{CT}_r$.)
16. Prover creates responses for $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}$. When asset-id is hidden, $\mathrm{at}$ is also a witness (11 elements). When revealed, $\mathrm{at}$ is public and its generator $G_3$ is dropped (10 elements):
    - **Asset-id hidden**:
    $$[\begin{matrix}
 \mathrm{sk}_r + \mathrm{sk} \cdot c, & \mathrm{bal}_{0_r} + \mathrm{bal}_0 \cdot c, & \mathrm{cnt}_r + \mathrm{cnt}_0 \cdot c, & \mathrm{at}_r + \mathrm{at} \cdot c, & \rho_r + \rho \cdot c, & \rho^i_r + \rho^i \cdot c, & s_r + s \cdot c, & s^i_r + s^i \cdot c, & \mathrm{id}_r + \mathrm{id} \cdot c, & \mathrm{ek}_r + \mathrm{ek} \cdot c, & b_{0_r} + b_0 \cdot c\\
 \end{matrix}]$$
    - **Asset-id revealed** (no $\mathrm{at}$ response; indices shift accordingly):
    $$[\begin{matrix}
 \mathrm{sk}_r + \mathrm{sk} \cdot c, & \mathrm{bal}_{0_r} + \mathrm{bal}_0 \cdot c, & \mathrm{cnt}_r + \mathrm{cnt}_0 \cdot c, & \rho_r + \rho \cdot c, & \rho^i_r + \rho^i \cdot c, & s_r + s \cdot c, & s^i_r + s^i \cdot c, & \mathrm{id}_r + \mathrm{id} \cdot c, & \mathrm{ek}_r + \mathrm{ek} \cdot c, & b_{0_r} + b_0 \cdot c\\
 \end{matrix}]$$
17. Prover creates responses for $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{new}}}$. Most witnesses are shared ($\bot$). If balance changes, create response for $\mathrm{bal}_1$. Using the same indexing as step 16 (the new state has no $b_0$ term, so the array has one fewer entry):
    $$[\begin{matrix}
 \bot, & \mathrm{bal}_{1_r} + \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot c \text{ or } \bot, & \bot, & \bot, & \bot, & \rho^{i+1}_r + \rho^{i+1} \cdot c, & \bot, & s^{i+1}_r + s^{i+1} \cdot c, & \bot, & \bot\\
 \end{matrix}]$$
    (Indices match step 16 hidden case: 0=$\mathrm{sk}$, 1=$\mathrm{bal}$, 2=$\mathrm{cnt}$, 3=$\mathrm{at}$ (hidden only), 4=$\rho$, 5=$\rho^i$, 6=$s$, 7=$s^i$, 8=$\mathrm{id}$, 9=$\mathrm{ek}$, 10=$b_0$)
18. Response for nullifier:
    $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{null}} = \rho^i_r + \rho^i \cdot c$$
19. For relation $C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\rho,s}}$, only the response for blinding $b'$ needs to be created:
    $\mathrm{resp}_{b'} = r_{\mathrm{BP}_{\rho,s}} + b' \cdot c$
20. For relation $C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}}$, only the response for blinding $b''$ is created here:
    $\mathrm{resp}_{b''} = r_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}} + b'' \cdot c$
    Its $v$ slot reuses the leg-link amount response $\mathrm{resp}_v$ produced in step 21.
21. The amount proof ($T_v$) and asset-id proof ($T_{\mathrm{at}}$) reuse shared responses; the only new response is for $v$:
    - $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ response: reused from $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{BP}_{\rho,s}}$ (the Bulletproof response at witness index 8). Used by both the amount proof and the asset-id proof.
    - $\mathrm{at}$ response (asset-id hidden case): reused from $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[3]$.
    - $v$ response: $\mathrm{resp}_v = v_r + v \cdot c$ is produced here by the amount proof. When the balance changes, the balance Bulletproof reuses this same $\mathrm{resp}_v$ for its $v$ slot, so the leg's encrypted amount equals the balance change.
22. The proof is
    $$
 (C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\rho,s}}, C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}}, \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}, \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}, \mathrm{Path}_r, N, T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}, T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}}, $$
    $$T_{\mathrm{null}}, T_{\mathrm{BP}_{\rho,s}}, T_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}}, T_{\mathrm{at}}, T_v, \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}, \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{new}}}, \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{null}}, $$
    $$\mathrm{resp}_{b'}, \mathrm{resp}_{b''}, \mathrm{resp}_{v})
 $$


#### Verifier

1. Verifier knows the transaction type $\mathrm{txn\_type}$ which determines counter direction and balance change expectations.
2. Verifier hashes the following to create challenge $c$ as:
   $$
 c = \mathrm{Hash}(\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}, \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}, N, \mathrm{Path}_r, \mathrm{Root}, \mathrm{txn\_type}, C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\rho,s}}, C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}}, $$
   $$T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}, T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}}, T_{\mathrm{null}}, T_{\mathrm{BP}_{\rho,s}}, T_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}}, T_{\mathrm{at}}, T_v)
 $$
3. Verifier enforces the Bulletproof constraints for the 2 multiplications: $\rho^{i+1} = \rho \cdot \rho^i$ and $s^{i+1} = s \cdot s^i$, and the inverse constraint $\mathrm{ek} \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1} = 1$.
4. Verifier enforces the Bulletproof constraint for the balance change based on $\mathrm{txn\_type}$:
    - If $\mathrm{txn\_type} \in \{\mathrm{Affirm}_s, \mathrm{Irr}-\mathrm{Affirm}_s\}$: $\mathrm{bal}_1 = \mathrm{bal}_0 - v$
    - If $\mathrm{txn\_type} \in \{\mathrm{Claim}_r, \mathrm{Reverse}_s, \mathrm{Irr}-\mathrm{Affirm}_r\}$: $\mathrm{bal}_1 = \mathrm{bal}_0 + v$
5. Verifier enforces the Bulletproof constraints for curve tree membership to verify that $\mathrm{Path}_r$ leads to $\mathrm{Root}$.
6. Verifies correctness of $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}$. When asset-id is hidden, $G_3$ is part of the commitment and all 11 responses are used. When asset-id is revealed, $\mathrm{at} \cdot G_3$ is subtracted from $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}$ (since $\mathrm{at}$ is public) and $G_3$ is dropped from the generator list, shifting indices 4+ down by one:
   - **Asset-id hidden** (indices 0–10 as in step 16 hidden case):
     $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[0] \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[1] \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[2] \cdot G_2 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[3] \cdot G_3 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[4] \cdot G_4 + $$
     $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[5] \cdot G_5 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[6] \cdot G_6 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[7] \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[8] \cdot G_8 + $$
     $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[9] \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[10] \cdot H_0 \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}} + \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r} \cdot c$$
   - **Asset-id revealed** (indices 0–9 as in step 16 revealed case; target adjusted by subtracting $\mathrm{at} \cdot G_3$):
     $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[0] \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[1] \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[2] \cdot G_2 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[3] \cdot G_4 + $$
     $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[4] \cdot G_5 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[5] \cdot G_6 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[6] \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[7] \cdot G_8 + $$
     $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[8] \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[9] \cdot H_0 \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}} + (\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r} - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3) \cdot c$$

7. Verifies correctness of $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}$ by checking (reusing responses for shared witnesses from $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}$). The same hidden/revealed distinction applies as in step 6. Letting $D_{\mathrm{new}}$ denote the adjusted $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}$ (i.e., $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}$ when hidden, or $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}} - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3$ when revealed):
    - If counter increased by 1: target is $D_{\mathrm{new}} - G_2$
    - If counter decreased by 1: target is $D_{\mathrm{new}} + G_2$
    - If counter remains same: target is $D_{\mathrm{new}}$

    The generator-to-response mapping follows step 16 (with $G_3$ and its response present or absent depending on whether asset-id is hidden or revealed). If balance doesn't change, $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{new}}}[1]$ is $\bot$ and verifier uses $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[1]$ for both old and new balance responses. Similarly, $s$ (index 6) is shared between old and new states.

8. Verifies correctness of nullifier by checking:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{null}} \cdot G_5 \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{null}} + N \cdot c$$
   $$\implies (\rho^i_r + \rho^i \cdot c) \cdot G_5 \stackrel{?}{=} \rho^i_r \cdot G_5 + N \cdot c$$

9. Verifier checks response for $C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\rho,s}}$ as:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{b'} \cdot H_0 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[4] \cdot H_1 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[5] \cdot H_2 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{new}}}[5] \cdot H_3 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[6] \cdot H_4 + $$
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[7] \cdot H_5 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{new}}}[7] \cdot H_6 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[9] \cdot H_7 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{BP}_{\rho,s}}[8] \cdot H_8 \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{BP}_{\rho,s}} + C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\rho,s}} \cdot c$$
   $$\implies (r_{\mathrm{BP}_{\rho,s}} + b' \cdot c) \cdot H_0 + (\rho_r + \rho \cdot c) \cdot H_1 + (\rho^i_r + \rho^i \cdot c) \cdot H_2 + (\rho^{i+1}_r + \rho^{i+1} \cdot c) \cdot H_3 + (s_r + s \cdot c) \cdot H_4 + $$
   $$(s^i_r + s^i \cdot c) \cdot H_5 + (s^{i+1}_r + s^{i+1} \cdot c) \cdot H_6 + (\mathrm{ek}_r + \mathrm{ek} \cdot c) \cdot H_7 + (\mathrm{ek}^{-1}_r + \mathrm{ek}^{-1} \cdot c) \cdot H_8 \stackrel{?}{=} $$
   $$r_{\mathrm{BP}_{\rho,s}} \cdot H_0 + \rho_r \cdot H_1 + \rho^i_r \cdot H_2 + \rho^{i+1}_r \cdot H_3 + s_r \cdot H_4 + s^i_r \cdot H_5 + s^{i+1}_r \cdot H_6 + \mathrm{ek}_r \cdot H_7 + \mathrm{ek}^{-1}_r \cdot H_8 + C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\rho,s}} \cdot c$$
   Note: indices 4, 5 refer to $\rho, \rho^i$ responses from $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}$ (hidden asset-id case), and index 9 to $\mathrm{ek}$. When asset-id is revealed, the $\rho, \rho^i, s, s^i, \mathrm{ek}$ indices each shift down by one (so $\rho, \rho^i$ are at 3, 4 and $\mathrm{ek}$ at 8).

10. Verifier checks response for $C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}}$ as:
    $$\mathrm{resp}_{b''} \cdot H_0 + \mathrm{resp}_v \cdot H_1 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[1] \cdot H_2 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{new}}}[1] \cdot H_3 \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}} + C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}} \cdot c$$
    $$\implies (r_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}} + b'' \cdot c) \cdot H_0 + (v_r + v \cdot c) \cdot H_1 + ( \mathrm{bal}_{0_r} + \mathrm{bal}_0 \cdot c) \cdot H_2 + ( \mathrm{bal}_{1_r} + \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot c) \cdot H_3 \stackrel{?}{=} r_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}} \cdot H_0 + $$
    $$v_r \cdot H_1 + \mathrm{bal}_{0_r} \cdot H_2 + \mathrm{bal}_{1_r} \cdot H_3 + C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}} \cdot c$$

11. Verifier checks the leg-account link using shared responses — no separate responses were created for $T_v$ or $T_{\mathrm{at}}$. Per leg:
    - **Amount (when the asset-id is revealed in this leg, or the balance changes)**, using $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{BP}_{\rho,s}}[8]$ for $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ and $\mathrm{resp}_v$ for $v$:
      $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{BP}_{\rho,s}}[8] \cdot \mathrm{Eph}_v + \mathrm{resp}_v \cdot H \stackrel{?}{=} T_v + \mathrm{CT}_v \cdot c$$
      ($\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{BP}_{\rho,s}}[8]$ is the $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ response from the Bulletproof commitment; $\mathrm{resp}_v$ is the amount response, which the balance Bulletproof reuses when the balance changes, so the leg's encrypted amount equals the balance change.)
    - **Asset-id (when the asset-id is encrypted in this leg):**
        - **Asset-id hidden**: using $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{BP}_{\rho,s}}[8]$ for $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ and $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[3]$ for $\mathrm{at}$:
          $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{BP}_{\rho,s}}[8] \cdot \mathrm{Eph}_{\mathrm{at}} + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[3] \cdot H \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{at}} + \mathrm{CT}_{\mathrm{at}} \cdot c$$
        - **Asset-id revealed but not in this leg**: using $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{BP}_{\rho,s}}[8]$ for $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$; $\mathrm{at}$ is public so the target is adjusted:
          $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{BP}_{\rho,s}}[8] \cdot \mathrm{Eph}_{\mathrm{at}} \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{at}} + (\mathrm{CT}_{\mathrm{at}} - H \cdot \mathrm{at}) \cdot c$$
        - **Asset-id revealed in this leg**: no asset-id check ($\mathrm{at}$ is public; steps 6–7 already subtract $\mathrm{at} \cdot G_3$ from the state targets).

12. Verifier finally checks the Bulletproofs proof for all constraints i.e. curve tree membership, multiplications, inverse relation, and balance change

### Design notes

**Why there is no participant ciphertext proof.** An earlier version of this protocol bound every leg to the account through a participant relation $\mathrm{CT}_s = \mathrm{Eph}_s \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1} + G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \cdot \mathrm{ek}$ (sender) or $\mathrm{CT}_r = \mathrm{Eph}_r \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1} + G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \cdot \mathrm{ek}$ (receiver), proved for every leg. That relation was dropped because it is a *weaker* binding. With $\mathrm{Eph}_s = G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \cdot \mathrm{sk}_s \cdot r_1$ and $\mathrm{CT}_s = G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \cdot (\mathrm{sk}_s + r_1)$ (where $r_1$ is the leg randomness and $\mathrm{sk}_s$ the sender's key), the relation $\mathrm{CT}_s = \mathrm{Eph}_s \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1} + G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \cdot \mathrm{ek}$ is satisfied by *two* values of $\mathrm{ek}$ — the real account key $\mathrm{sk}_s$ and the leg randomness $r_1$ — because both the $\mathrm{ek}$ and $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ terms live in $\langle G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \rangle$. For a leg that reveals its asset-id and does not change the balance, the participant relation was the *only* tie to the account, so a prover could satisfy it with $\mathrm{ek} = r_1$ instead of the account key. The amount ciphertext $\mathrm{CT}_v = \mathrm{Eph}_v \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1} + H \cdot v$ carries its plaintext on a *different* generator $H$, which pins $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ to a single value; the account is therefore now bound to such a leg through $\mathrm{CT}_v$ (proved even with no balance change), and to legs that encrypt their asset-id through $\mathrm{CT}_{\mathrm{at}}$ — see step 11.

**Committing $\mathrm{ek}$ rather than $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ in the account state.** Twisted-ElGamal decryption and every per-leg ciphertext relation use $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ (e.g. $\mathrm{CT} - \mathrm{Eph} \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1}$). To make $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ available as a witness bound to the account, the protocol commits $\mathrm{ek}$ in the account state (as the public key $\mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$) and *additionally* commits $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ in the Bulletproof, enforcing $\mathrm{ek} \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1} = 1$ with one multiplication; the $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ response is then reused across the ciphertext relations. An earlier version instead committed $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ directly in the account state, avoiding the extra committed value and the inverse constraint. That was safe: exposing $g^{\mathrm{ek}^{-1}}$ alongside the public key $g^{\mathrm{ek}}$ is the Inverse Decisional Diffie-Hellman problem (InvDDH), and Bao, Deng and Zhu (["Variations of the Diffie-Hellman Problem", 2003](https://ink.library.smu.edu.sg/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?params=/context/sis_research/article/2082/&path_info=Bao2003_VariationsOfDiffie_HellmanProblem_pv.pdf), §3.1–3.2) show $\text{InvDDH} \Leftarrow \text{SDDH} \Leftarrow \text{DDH}$ (SDDH = square DDH) — i.e. InvDDH is implied by the DDH assumption the scheme already needs for the semantic security of its Twisted-ElGamal ciphertexts, so it introduces no new assumption.

### Split prover variant (W2/W3)

In W2/W3 the work is split between a host (which does not know $\mathrm{sk}, \mathrm{ek}$) and a device (which holds $\mathrm{sk}, \mathrm{ek}$). Since $\mathrm{AK}$ and $\mathrm{EK}$ 
can't be revealed, the verifier reconstructs $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}$ and $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}$ from device partials and host partials that sum to them. The leg 
ciphertexts whose plaintexts are private (amount, and asset-id in the "hidden" sub-case) are also split between device and host with cancelling $\pm k$ blindings — the sum check enforces that device and host used the same $k$.

The $\mathrm{ek} \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1} = 1$ relation is no longer enforced via Bulletproof constraints. Instead the device proves it via a Sigma protocol on a fresh element $D$ (described below), and the $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ response from that proof is the bridging element reused across all per-leg ciphertext checks on the device side.

#### Account commitment split

The device picks fresh independent randomness shares $r_{\mathrm{old},D}, r_{\mathrm{new},D} \in \mathbb{Z}_p$ and publishes:
$$P_{\mathrm{old},D} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + r_{\mathrm{old},D} \cdot H_0$$
$$P_{\mathrm{new},D} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + r_{\mathrm{new},D} \cdot H_0$$
The host's complementary partials are (asset-id hidden case shown; when asset-id is revealed, the $\mathrm{at} \cdot G_3$ term is omitted and added back as public by the verifier):
$$P_{\mathrm{old},H} = \mathrm{bal}_0 \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{cnt}_0 \cdot G_2 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + \rho^i \cdot G_5 + s \cdot G_6 + s^i \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8 + r_{\mathrm{old},H} \cdot H_0$$
$$P_{\mathrm{new},H} = \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{cnt}_1 \cdot G_2 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + \rho^{i+1} \cdot G_5 + s \cdot G_6 + s^{i+1} \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8 + r_{\mathrm{new},H} \cdot H_0$$
with $r_{\mathrm{old},D} + r_{\mathrm{old},H} = b_0$ (so old partials sum to $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r} = \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}} + b_0 \cdot H_0$) and $r_{\mathrm{new},D} + r_{\mathrm{new},H} = 0$ (so new partials sum to $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}$, which has no $H_0$ term). The host knows $r_{\mathrm{old},H}, r_{\mathrm{new},H}$; the device knows $r_{\mathrm{old},D}, r_{\mathrm{new},D}$. The two parties agree on these shares before proving — typically the device picks $r_{\mathrm{old},D}, r_{\mathrm{new},D}$ and the host derives its complementary values.

#### Device prover

1. **Account commitment partials.** Pick fresh blindings $\mathrm{sk}_r, \mathrm{ek}_r, r_{\mathrm{old},D_r}, r_{\mathrm{new},D_r} \in \mathbb{Z}_p$, reusing $\mathrm{sk}_r, \mathrm{ek}_r$ for both partials:
   $$T_{P_{\mathrm{old},D}} = \mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{ek}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + r_{\mathrm{old},D_r} \cdot H_0$$
   $$T_{P_{\mathrm{new},D}} = \mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{ek}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + r_{\mathrm{new},D_r} \cdot H_0$$
2. **D-relation** (replaces the $\mathrm{ek} \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1} = 1$ BP constraint). Pick fresh $r \in \mathbb{Z}_p$ and compute
   $$D = G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \cdot \mathrm{ek} + H_0 \cdot r$$
   The blinding $r$ hides $\mathrm{ek}$ in $D$. The device proves two things about $D$:
   - **$D$ opening.** Knowledge of $(\mathrm{ek}, r)$ in $D = G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \cdot \mathrm{ek} + H_0 \cdot r$, reusing the account partials' $\mathrm{ek}_r$ (step 1) so the $\mathrm{ek}$ response $s_{\mathrm{ek}}$ is shared. Pick a blinding $r_r$:
     $$T_D = G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \cdot \mathrm{ek}_r + H_0 \cdot r_r$$
     The shared $s_{\mathrm{ek}}$ binds the $\mathrm{ek}$ inside $D$ to the account's $\mathrm{ek}$; without it the device could open $D$ with an arbitrary key.
   - **Inverse relation.** Since $G_{\mathrm{Enc}} = D \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1} + H_0 \cdot (-r \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1})$, knowledge of $(\mathrm{ek}^{-1}, -r \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1})$ in it. Pick blindings $(\mathrm{ek}^{-1})_r, (-r \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1})_r$:
     $$T_{G_{\mathrm{Enc}}} = D \cdot (\mathrm{ek}^{-1})_r + H_0 \cdot (-r \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1})_r$$
     Given $D$ opens to the account's $\mathrm{ek}$, this forces $\mathrm{ek} \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1} = 1$, so $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ is the true inverse. The $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ response is reused as the bridging element in all per-leg ciphertext checks below.
3. **Per-leg amount partial** (when the asset-id is revealed in this leg, or the balance changes). Pick fresh $k_{\mathrm{amt}} \in \mathbb{Z}_p$ and a blinding $k_{\mathrm{amt},r}$, publish
   $$P_{\mathrm{ct}_{\mathrm{amt}},D} = H_0 \cdot k_{\mathrm{amt}} + \mathrm{eph\_pk}_{\mathrm{amt}} \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1}$$
   $$T_{P_{\mathrm{ct}_{\mathrm{amt}},D}} = H_0 \cdot k_{\mathrm{amt},r} + \mathrm{eph\_pk}_{\mathrm{amt}} \cdot (\mathrm{ek}^{-1})_r$$
4. **Per-leg asset-id:**
   - **Hidden**: pick fresh $k_{\mathrm{at}} \in \mathbb{Z}_p$ and a blinding $k_{\mathrm{at},r}$, publish
     $$P_{\mathrm{ct\_at},D} = H_0 \cdot k_{\mathrm{at}} + \mathrm{eph\_pk}_{\mathrm{asset\_id}} \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1}$$
     $$T_{P_{\mathrm{ct\_at},D}} = H_0 \cdot k_{\mathrm{at},r} + \mathrm{eph\_pk}_{\mathrm{asset\_id}} \cdot (\mathrm{ek}^{-1})_r$$
   - **Revealed elsewhere**: $\mathrm{at}$ is public so no $k$ is needed. Prove discrete log of $(\mathrm{CT}_{\mathrm{at}} - \mathrm{at} \cdot H)$ over $\mathrm{eph\_pk}_{\mathrm{asset\_id}}$ with witness $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ by reusing $(\mathrm{ek}^{-1})_r$:
     $$T_{\mathrm{at},\mathrm{DL}} = \mathrm{eph\_pk}_{\mathrm{asset\_id}} \cdot (\mathrm{ek}^{-1})_r$$
   - **Revealed in this leg**: nothing to prove for this leg's asset-id.
5. Device hashes its transcript — containing $\mathrm{nonce}$, $P_{\mathrm{old},D}, P_{\mathrm{new},D}, D$, the per-leg partials $P_{\mathrm{ct}_{\mathrm{amt}},D}, P_{\mathrm{ct\_at},D}$, the leg encryptions, and all $T$ values above — to derive challenge $c$.
6. Device responses:
   - $s_{\mathrm{sk}} = \mathrm{sk}_r + \mathrm{sk} \cdot c$ (shared across both account partials)
   - $s_{\mathrm{ek}} = \mathrm{ek}_r + \mathrm{ek} \cdot c$ (shared across both account partials and the $D$ opening)
   - $s_{r_{\mathrm{old},D}} = r_{\mathrm{old},D_r} + r_{\mathrm{old},D} \cdot c$, $\quad s_{r_{\mathrm{new},D}} = r_{\mathrm{new},D_r} + r_{\mathrm{new},D} \cdot c$
   - $s_r = r_r + r \cdot c$ (for the $D$ opening)
   - $s_{\mathrm{ek}^{-1}} = (\mathrm{ek}^{-1})_r + \mathrm{ek}^{-1} \cdot c$ (shared across the inverse relation, the per-leg amount partial, and the per-leg asset-id)
   - $s_{-r \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1}} = (-r \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1})_r + (-r \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1}) \cdot c$
   - Per leg with a $\mathrm{ct}_{\mathrm{amt}}$ partial: $s_{k_{\mathrm{amt}}} = k_{\mathrm{amt},r} + k_{\mathrm{amt}} \cdot c$
   - Per leg with hidden asset-id: $s_{k_{\mathrm{at}}} = k_{\mathrm{at},r} + k_{\mathrm{at}} \cdot c$

#### Device verifier

The verifier recomputes $c$ from the device's transcript and checks each Sigma relation. Each check has the standard structure $\text{(linear combination of responses)} \stackrel{?}{=} T + \text{(committed value)}.c$:
- $s_{\mathrm{sk}} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + s_{\mathrm{ek}} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + s_{r_{\mathrm{old},D}} \cdot H_0 \stackrel{?}{=} T_{P_{\mathrm{old},D}} + P_{\mathrm{old},D} \cdot c$
- $s_{\mathrm{sk}} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + s_{\mathrm{ek}} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + s_{r_{\mathrm{new},D}} \cdot H_0 \stackrel{?}{=} T_{P_{\mathrm{new},D}} + P_{\mathrm{new},D} \cdot c$
- $D$ opening: $s_{\mathrm{ek}} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + s_r \cdot H_0 \stackrel{?}{=} T_D + D \cdot c$
- Inverse relation: $s_{\mathrm{ek}^{-1}} \cdot D + s_{-r \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1}} \cdot H_0 \stackrel{?}{=} T_{G_{\mathrm{Enc}}} + G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \cdot c$
- Per leg with a $\mathrm{ct}_{\mathrm{amt}}$ partial (asset-id revealed in this leg, or balance changes): $s_{\mathrm{ek}^{-1}} \cdot \mathrm{eph\_pk}_{\mathrm{amt}} + s_{k_{\mathrm{amt}}} \cdot H_0 \stackrel{?}{=} T_{P_{\mathrm{ct}_{\mathrm{amt}},D}} + P_{\mathrm{ct}_{\mathrm{amt}},D} \cdot c$
- Per leg with hidden asset-id: $s_{\mathrm{ek}^{-1}} \cdot \mathrm{eph\_pk}_{\mathrm{asset\_id}} + s_{k_{\mathrm{at}}} \cdot H_0 \stackrel{?}{=} T_{P_{\mathrm{ct\_at},D}} + P_{\mathrm{ct\_at},D} \cdot c$
- Per leg with asset-id revealed elsewhere: $s_{\mathrm{ek}^{-1}} \cdot \mathrm{eph\_pk}_{\mathrm{asset\_id}} \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{at},\mathrm{DL}} + (\mathrm{CT}_{\mathrm{at}} - \mathrm{at} \cdot H) \cdot c$

Reusing $s_{\mathrm{sk}}, s_{\mathrm{ek}}, s_{\mathrm{ek}^{-1}}$ across multiple checks binds the same witnesses across them.

#### Host side (differences from W1)

The host runs the W1 prover above with the following changes:

- $\mathrm{sk}, \mathrm{ek}$ are dropped from the witness set. The $\mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{ek}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$ terms are dropped from $T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}$ and $T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}}$, and the corresponding responses are dropped.
- The host's targets are $P_{\mathrm{old},H}$ and $P_{\mathrm{new},H}$ in place of $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}$ and $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}$. The verifier reconstructs them as $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r} - P_{\mathrm{old},D}$ and $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}} - P_{\mathrm{new},D}$ (adding back $\mathrm{at} \cdot G_3$ as public when asset-id is revealed).
- $\mathrm{ek}$ and $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ are dropped from $C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\rho,s}}$, along with the $\mathrm{ek} \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1} = 1$ Bulletproof constraint. The BP commitment now contains only $(\rho, \rho^i, \rho^{i+1}, s, s^i, s^{i+1})$ with the two multiplication constraints for randomness evolution.
- The leg-link terms ($T_{\mathrm{at}}$ and $T_v$) and their shared responses are dropped from the host side — these live on the device. In their place:
  - For each leg with a $\mathrm{ct}_{\mathrm{amt}}$ partial (asset-id revealed in this leg, or balance changes), host publishes
    $$P_{\mathrm{ct}_{\mathrm{amt}},H} = H \cdot v + H_0 \cdot (-k_{\mathrm{amt}})$$
    and proves knowledge of $(v, -k_{\mathrm{amt}})$ in it. The same $k_{\mathrm{amt}}$ as the device's is used with the sign flipped — the device and host agree on this value before proving. When the balance changes, the $v$ response is shared with the balance BP's $v$ response.
  - For each leg with hidden asset-id, host publishes
    $$P_{\mathrm{ct\_at},H} = H \cdot \mathrm{at} + H_0 \cdot (-k_{\mathrm{at}})$$
    and proves knowledge of $(\mathrm{at}, -k_{\mathrm{at}})$ in it. Same coordination of $k_{\mathrm{at}}$ with the device. The $\mathrm{at}$ response is shared with the host's account state Schnorr's $\mathrm{at}$ response.
- The randomness shares $r_{\mathrm{old},H}, r_{\mathrm{new},H}$ get their own blindings in $T_{P_{\mathrm{old},H}}$ and $T_{P_{\mathrm{new},H}}$ (replacing the $b_0$ blinding in W1's $T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}$).

Nullifier, curve tree membership, randomness powers BP and its multiplication constraints, balance BP and its $\mathrm{bal}_1 = \mathrm{bal}_0 \pm v$ constraint, and the $\mathrm{bal}_1 \leq \mathrm{MAX\_BALANCE}$ range proof are all unchanged.

#### Verifier additions

In addition to running the device verifier and the host's state/nullifier/BP checks, the verifier:

- Reconstructs $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r} \stackrel{?}{=} P_{\mathrm{old},D} + P_{\mathrm{old},H}$ and $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}} \stackrel{?}{=} P_{\mathrm{new},D} + P_{\mathrm{new},H}$ (with $\mathrm{at} \cdot G_3$ added back to the host partials when asset-id is revealed).
- For each leg with a $\mathrm{ct}_{\mathrm{amt}}$ partial (asset-id revealed in this leg, or balance changes): $\mathrm{ct}_{\mathrm{amt}} \stackrel{?}{=} P_{\mathrm{ct}_{\mathrm{amt}},D} + P_{\mathrm{ct}_{\mathrm{amt}},H}$.
- For each leg with hidden asset-id: $\mathrm{CT}_{\mathrm{at}} \stackrel{?}{=} P_{\mathrm{ct\_at},D} + P_{\mathrm{ct\_at},H}$.

These sum checks implicitly enforce that the device and host used the same $k_{\mathrm{amt}}$ (resp. $k_{\mathrm{at}}$), since otherwise the $H_0$ contributions wouldn't cancel.

#### Binding host and device

**W2 (parallel).** The device's $\mathrm{nonce}$ is the same $\mathrm{nonce}$ the host uses, so both proofs end up bound to the same context. Device and host coordinate on the randomness shares ($r_{\mathrm{old},D}, r_{\mathrm{new},D}$ and per leg $k_{\mathrm{amt}}, k_{\mathrm{at}}$) before proving, since the verifier's sum checks require those values to line up; the two proofs are then produced and verified independently.

**W3 (sequential).** The host first runs its pre-challenge phase of the host prover above — picking all blindings and computing all host-side $T$ values, with $\mathrm{sk}$- and $\mathrm{ek}$-related parts dropped as described above. It then derives its challenge from that transcript exactly as the W1 challenge $c$ is derived but with the dropped $T$s omitted from the hash; call this challenge $c_H$. The host sends $c_H$ to the device. The device sets its $\mathrm{nonce}$ to
$$\mathrm{nonce}' = c_H \| \mathrm{nonce}$$
(concatenation of the byte serialization of $c_H$ and the original $\mathrm{nonce}$), runs the Device prover above against $\mathrm{nonce}'$, and sends its full proof back to the host. The host appends the device proof bytes to its transcript and derives a fresh challenge
$$c_H' = \mathrm{Hash}(c_H, \text{device proof bytes})$$
which it uses for its own response generation. The verifier mirrors the same ordering: derives $c_H$, reconstructs $\mathrm{nonce}' = c_H \| \mathrm{nonce}$, verifies the device proof against $\mathrm{nonce}'$, then derives $c_H'$ from the device proof bytes and verifies the host's responses against $c_H'$.


## Proof of balance

An investor can be asked for proof of balance of certain account (of certain asset-id). 
The requester of this proof might be the auditor who has visibility of the pending settlements, or it 
might be someone else who the investor should prove that the sum of its finalized balance and balance 
in pending settlements is certain value. The prover's public key, asset-id and balance are revealed to the verifier. 
The investor also reveals the nullifier of the account to prove that it is using the latest account state as the verifier 
can check that nullifier has not been revealed. An improvement could be to prove knowledge of an unrevealed nullifier using a 
proof of non-membership in the nullifier set (nullifier set has to be an accumulator). 
The prover should share the index of account commitment in tree so verifier can efficiently fetch the commitment and compare. 
If its not possible then do a membership proof. Prover could hide the commitment by randomizing it with a new blinding ($\mathrm{State}' = \mathrm{State} + B \cdot r'$)

There are 2 variants of this proof:

1. **Proof of balance with auditor**: The verifier is an auditor who already has visibility of the account state including the counter. The prover only needs to prove correctness of the public key and nullifier against the account commitment.
2. **Proof of balance with non-auditor**: The verifier is an arbitrary party who may not have visibility of pending transactions. The prover must prove the counter equals the number of legs provided, prove their role in each leg, and prove the sum of pending amounts.

### Proof of balance with auditor

In this protocol, the verifier is an auditor who already has access to the account commitment and knows the counter value. Both public keys $\mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Aff}}$ and $\mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Enc}}$ are revealed (the auditor knows the account). The prover must prove:

1. The secret key $\mathrm{sk}$ in the account commitment corresponds to the revealed $\mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Aff}}$.
2. The encryption secret key $\mathrm{ek}$ in the account commitment corresponds to the revealed $\mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Enc}}$.
3. The nullifier $N$ is correctly derived from $\rho^i$ in the account commitment.

**Instance**: $\mathrm{State}, \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Aff}}, \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Enc}}, \mathrm{at}, \mathrm{balance}, \mathrm{counter}, \mathrm{id}, N, G_{\mathrm{Aff}}, G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$

**Witness**: $\mathrm{sk}, \mathrm{ek}, \rho, \rho^i, s, s^i$

#### Prover

1. Prover wants to prove knowledge of $\rho, \rho^i, s, s^i$ in the relation:
   $$\rho \cdot G_4 + \rho^i \cdot G_5 + s \cdot G_6 + s^i \cdot G_7 = \mathrm{State} - \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Aff}} - \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Enc}} - \mathrm{balance} \cdot G_1 - \mathrm{counter} \cdot G_2 - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 - \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8$$
   where the RHS is public.
2. Prover wants to prove knowledge of $\rho^i$ in $N = \rho^i \cdot G_5$.
3. Prover wants to prove knowledge of $\mathrm{sk}$ in $\mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Aff}} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}$.
4. Prover wants to prove knowledge of $\mathrm{ek}$ in $\mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Enc}} = \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$.
5. Prover picks random $\rho_r, \rho^i_r, s_r, s^i_r, \mathrm{sk}_r, \mathrm{ek}_r \in \mathbb{Z}_p$ and creates:
   $$T_{\mathrm{acc}} = \rho_r \cdot G_4 + \rho^i_r \cdot G_5 + s_r \cdot G_6 + s^i_r \cdot G_7, \in \mathbb{G}_p$$
   $$T_{\mathrm{null}} = \rho^i_r \cdot G_5, \in \mathbb{G}_p$$
   $$T_{\mathrm{pk}} = \mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}, \in \mathbb{G}_p$$
   $$T_{\mathrm{EK}} = \mathrm{ek}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}, \in \mathbb{G}_p$$
6. Prover hashes the following to create challenge $c$ as:
   $$c = \mathrm{Hash}(\mathrm{State}, \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Aff}}, \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Enc}}, \mathrm{at}, \mathrm{balance}, \mathrm{counter}, \mathrm{id}, N, T_{\mathrm{acc}}, T_{\mathrm{null}}, T_{\mathrm{pk}}, T_{\mathrm{EK}})$$
7. Prover creates responses:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{acc}} = [\begin{matrix}
 \rho_r + \rho \cdot c, & \rho^i_r + \rho^i \cdot c, & s_r + s \cdot c, & s^i_r + s^i \cdot c\\
 \end{matrix}]$$
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{pk}} = \mathrm{sk}_r + \mathrm{sk} \cdot c$$
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{EK}} = \mathrm{ek}_r + \mathrm{ek} \cdot c$$
8. The proof is
   $$(N, T_{\mathrm{acc}}, T_{\mathrm{null}}, T_{\mathrm{pk}}, T_{\mathrm{EK}}, \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{acc}}, \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{pk}}, \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{EK}})$$

#### Verifier

1. Verifier hashes the following to create challenge $c$ as:
   $$c = \mathrm{Hash}(\mathrm{State}, \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Aff}}, \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Enc}}, \mathrm{at}, \mathrm{balance}, \mathrm{counter}, \mathrm{id}, N, T_{\mathrm{acc}}, T_{\mathrm{null}}, T_{\mathrm{pk}}, T_{\mathrm{EK}})$$
2. Verifies correctness of account state by checking:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{acc}}[0] \cdot G_4 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{acc}}[1] \cdot G_5 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{acc}}[2] \cdot G_6 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{acc}}[3] \cdot G_7 \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{acc}} + (\mathrm{State} - \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Aff}} - \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Enc}} - \mathrm{balance} \cdot G_1 - \mathrm{counter} \cdot G_2 - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 - \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8) \cdot c$$
   $$\implies (\rho_r + \rho \cdot c) \cdot G_4 + (\rho^i_r + \rho^i \cdot c) \cdot G_5 + (s_r + s \cdot c) \cdot G_6 + (s^i_r + s^i \cdot c) \cdot G_7 \stackrel{?}{=} \rho_r \cdot G_4 + \rho^i_r \cdot G_5 + s_r \cdot G_6 + $$
   $$s^i_r \cdot G_7 + (\mathrm{State} - \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Aff}} - \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Enc}} - \mathrm{balance} \cdot G_1 - \mathrm{counter} \cdot G_2 - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 - \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8) \cdot c$$
3. Verifies correctness of nullifier by checking:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{acc}}[1] \cdot G_5 \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{null}} + N \cdot c$$
   $$\implies (\rho^i_r + \rho^i \cdot c) \cdot G_5 \stackrel{?}{=} \rho^i_r \cdot G_5 + N \cdot c$$
4. Verifies knowledge of affirmation secret key by checking:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{pk}} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{pk}} + \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Aff}} \cdot c$$
   $$\implies (\mathrm{sk}_r + \mathrm{sk} \cdot c) \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} \stackrel{?}{=} \mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Aff}} \cdot c$$
5. Verifies knowledge of encryption secret key by checking:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{EK}} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{EK}} + \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Enc}} \cdot c$$
   $$\implies (\mathrm{ek}_r + \mathrm{ek} \cdot c) \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \stackrel{?}{=} \mathrm{ek}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Enc}} \cdot c$$

### Proof of balance with non-auditor

In this protocol, the verifier is an arbitrary party who may not have visibility of the pending transactions. The prover provides all pending leg encryptions and must prove:

1. The counter equals the number of legs provided.
2. The nullifier $N = \rho^i \cdot G_5$ is correctly derived from the account commitment (so the verifier can check that the account state being referenced is the current, not-yet-invalidated one).
3. For each leg $k$, the prover has the correct role (sender or receiver). This is proven via the participant ciphertext: $\mathrm{ct}_{\mathrm{participant},k} - \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Enc}} = \mathrm{eph\_pk}_{\mathrm{participant},k} \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1}$, where $\mathrm{eph\_pk}_{\mathrm{participant},k}$ is $r_1$ if the prover is the sender of leg $k$ and $r_2$ if the prover is the receiver. Only the holder of $\mathrm{ek}$ can satisfy this relation with their own $\mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Enc}}$.
4. For each leg $k$, the asset-id matches the account's asset-id $\mathrm{at}$:
    - If leg $k$ has $\mathrm{at}$ revealed (`reveal_asset_id = true` for that leg), the verifier checks the revealed value directly against $\mathrm{at}$ and no Sigma proof is needed.
    - If leg $k$ hides $\mathrm{at}$, the prover proves $\mathrm{CT}_{\mathrm{at}_k} - \mathrm{at} \cdot H = \mathrm{eph\_pk}_{\mathrm{at},k} \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1}$, where $\mathrm{eph\_pk}_{\mathrm{at},k}$ is the prover's ephemeral key for the asset-id ciphertext in that leg.
5. The sum of amounts in legs where prover is sender equals the declared pending sent amount. Let $S$ be the leg indices where prover is sender. The prover proves $\sum_{k \in S} \mathrm{CT}_{v_k} - \mathrm{pending\_sent} \cdot H = (\sum_{k \in S} \mathrm{eph\_pk}_{\mathrm{amt},s_k}) \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1}$.
6. The sum of amounts in legs where prover is receiver equals the declared pending received amount. Let $R$ be the leg indices where prover is receiver. The prover proves $\sum_{k \in R} \mathrm{CT}_{v_k} - \mathrm{pending\_recv} \cdot H = (\sum_{k \in R} \mathrm{eph\_pk}_{\mathrm{amt},r_k}) \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1}$.

**Instance**: $\mathrm{State}, \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Aff}}, \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Enc}}, \mathrm{at}, \mathrm{balance}, \mathrm{counter}, \mathrm{id}, N, [\mathrm{Leg}_k], \mathrm{pending\_sent}, \mathrm{pending\_recv}, G_{\mathrm{Aff}}, G_{\mathrm{Enc}}, H$

**Witness**: $\mathrm{sk}, \mathrm{ek}, \mathrm{ek}^{-1}, \rho, \rho^i, s, s^i$

Here $\mathrm{Leg}_k$ denotes the leg encryption for the $k$-th pending leg (including its ciphertexts and ephemeral keys). Both $\mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Aff}}$ and $\mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Enc}}$ are subtracted as public.

#### Prover

1. Prover wants to prove knowledge of $\rho, \rho^i, s, s^i$ in the account commitment relation:
   $$\rho \cdot G_4 + \rho^i \cdot G_5 + s \cdot G_6 + s^i \cdot G_7 = \mathrm{State} - \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Aff}} - \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Enc}} - \mathrm{balance} \cdot G_1 - \mathrm{counter} \cdot G_2 - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 - \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8$$
2. Prover wants to prove knowledge of $\rho^i$ in $N = \rho^i \cdot G_5$ and $\mathrm{sk}$ in $\mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Aff}} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}$.
3. Prover proves knowledge of $\mathrm{ek}$ in $\mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Enc}} = \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$ and of $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ in $G_{\mathrm{Enc}} = \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Enc}} \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1}$. The second relation proves $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ is the true inverse of $\mathrm{ek}$ since $\mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Enc}} \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1} = G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \cdot \mathrm{ek} \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1} = G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$. The same blinding for $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ is reused across all the ciphertext relations below.
4. For each leg $k$, prove knowledge of $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ in the participant ciphertext:
   $$\mathrm{ct}_{\mathrm{participant},k} - \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Enc}} = \mathrm{ek}^{-1} \cdot \mathrm{eph\_pk}_{\mathrm{participant},k}$$
5. For each leg $k$ with hidden asset-id, prove knowledge of $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ in the asset-id ciphertext:
   $$\mathrm{CT}_{\mathrm{at}_k} - \mathrm{at} \cdot H = \mathrm{ek}^{-1} \cdot \mathrm{eph\_pk}_{\mathrm{at},k}$$
6. If $S$ is not empty, prove knowledge of $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ in the sum of sent amounts:
   $$\sum_{k \in S} \mathrm{CT}_{v_k} - \mathrm{pending\_sent} \cdot H = \mathrm{ek}^{-1} \cdot \sum_{k \in S} \mathrm{eph\_pk}_{\mathrm{amt},s_k}$$
7. If $R$ is not empty, prove knowledge of $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ in the sum of received amounts:
   $$\sum_{k \in R} \mathrm{CT}_{v_k} - \mathrm{pending\_recv} \cdot H = \mathrm{ek}^{-1} \cdot \sum_{k \in R} \mathrm{eph\_pk}_{\mathrm{amt},r_k}$$
8. Prover picks random $\rho_r, \rho^i_r, s_r, s^i_r, \mathrm{sk}_r, \mathrm{ek}_r, \mathrm{ek}^{-1}_r \in \mathbb{Z}_p$ and creates:
   $$T_{\mathrm{acc}} = \rho_r \cdot G_4 + \rho^i_r \cdot G_5 + s_r \cdot G_6 + s^i_r \cdot G_7$$
   $$T_{\mathrm{null}} = \rho^i_r \cdot G_5$$
   $$T_{\mathrm{pk}} = \mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}$$
   $$T_{\mathrm{EK}} = \mathrm{ek}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$$
   $$T_{G_{\mathrm{Enc}}} = \mathrm{ek}^{-1}_r \cdot \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Enc}}$$
   For each leg $k$: $T_{\mathrm{participant}_k} = \mathrm{ek}^{-1}_r \cdot \mathrm{eph\_pk}_{\mathrm{participant},k}$
   For each leg $k$ with hidden asset-id: $T_{\mathrm{at}_k} = \mathrm{ek}^{-1}_r \cdot \mathrm{eph\_pk}_{\mathrm{at},k}$
   $$T_{\mathrm{sent}} = \mathrm{ek}^{-1}_r \cdot \sum_{k \in S} \mathrm{eph\_pk}_{\mathrm{amt},s_k} \quad \text{(if } S \neq \emptyset\text{)}$$
   $$T_{\mathrm{recv}} = \mathrm{ek}^{-1}_r \cdot \sum_{k \in R} \mathrm{eph\_pk}_{\mathrm{amt},r_k} \quad \text{(if } R \neq \emptyset\text{)}$$
9. Prover hashes the following to create challenge $c$ as:
   $$c = \mathrm{Hash}(\mathrm{State}, \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Aff}}, \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Enc}}, \mathrm{at}, \mathrm{balance}, \mathrm{counter}, \mathrm{id}, N, [\mathrm{Leg}_k], \mathrm{pending\_sent}, \mathrm{pending\_recv},$$
   $$T_{\mathrm{acc}}, T_{\mathrm{null}}, T_{\mathrm{pk}}, T_{\mathrm{EK}}, T_{G_{\mathrm{Enc}}}, [T_{\mathrm{participant}_k}], [T_{\mathrm{at}_k}], T_{\mathrm{sent}}, T_{\mathrm{recv}})$$
10. Prover creates responses:
    $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{acc}} = [\begin{matrix}
 \rho_r + \rho \cdot c, & \rho^i_r + \rho^i \cdot c, & s_r + s \cdot c, & s^i_r + s^i \cdot c\\
 \end{matrix}]$$
    $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{pk}} = \mathrm{sk}_r + \mathrm{sk} \cdot c$$
    $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{EK}} = \mathrm{ek}_r + \mathrm{ek} \cdot c$$
    $$\mathrm{resp}_{G_{\mathrm{Enc}}} = \mathrm{ek}^{-1}_r + \mathrm{ek}^{-1} \cdot c$$
    No additional responses are created for $T_{\mathrm{participant}_k}, T_{\mathrm{at}_k}, T_{\mathrm{sent}}, T_{\mathrm{recv}}$ — they all reuse $\mathrm{resp}_{G_{\mathrm{Enc}}}$ (the $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ response) at verification time.
11. The proof is
    $$(N, T_{\mathrm{acc}}, T_{\mathrm{null}}, T_{\mathrm{pk}}, T_{\mathrm{EK}}, T_{G_{\mathrm{Enc}}}, [T_{\mathrm{participant}_k}], [T_{\mathrm{at}_k}], T_{\mathrm{sent}}, T_{\mathrm{recv}}, \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{acc}}, \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{pk}}, \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{EK}}, \mathrm{resp}_{G_{\mathrm{Enc}}})$$

#### Verifier

1. Verifier reconstructs the challenge $c$ as
   $$c = \mathrm{Hash}(\mathrm{State}, \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Aff}}, \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Enc}}, \mathrm{at}, \mathrm{balance}, \mathrm{counter}, \mathrm{id}, N, [\mathrm{Leg}_k], \mathrm{pending\_sent}, \mathrm{pending\_recv},$$
   $$T_{\mathrm{acc}}, T_{\mathrm{null}}, T_{\mathrm{pk}}, T_{\mathrm{EK}}, T_{G_{\mathrm{Enc}}}, [T_{\mathrm{participant}_k}], [T_{\mathrm{at}_k}], T_{\mathrm{sent}}, T_{\mathrm{recv}})$$
2. Verifier checks that $\mathrm{counter}$ equals the number of legs provided, and that for any leg $k$ which reveals its asset-id, the revealed value equals $\mathrm{at}$.
3. Verifies correctness of account state by checking:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{acc}}[0] \cdot G_4 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{acc}}[1] \cdot G_5 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{acc}}[2] \cdot G_6 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{acc}}[3] \cdot G_7 \stackrel{?}{=}$$
   $$T_{\mathrm{acc}} + (\mathrm{State} - \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Aff}} - \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Enc}} - \mathrm{balance} \cdot G_1 - \mathrm{counter} \cdot G_2 - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 - \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8) \cdot c$$
   $$\implies (\rho_r + \rho \cdot c) \cdot G_4 + (\rho^i_r + \rho^i \cdot c) \cdot G_5 + (s_r + s \cdot c) \cdot G_6 + (s^i_r + s^i \cdot c) \cdot G_7 \stackrel{?}{=} \rho_r \cdot G_4 + \rho^i_r \cdot G_5 + s_r \cdot G_6 + $$
   $$s^i_r \cdot G_7 + (\mathrm{State} - \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Aff}} - \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Enc}} - \mathrm{balance} \cdot G_1 - \mathrm{counter} \cdot G_2 - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 - \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8) \cdot c$$
4. Verifies correctness of nullifier by checking:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{acc}}[1] \cdot G_5 \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{null}} + N \cdot c$$
   $$\implies (\rho^i_r + \rho^i \cdot c) \cdot G_5 \stackrel{?}{=} \rho^i_r \cdot G_5 + N \cdot c$$
   Note that verifier uses $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{acc}}[1]$ for checking both the account commitment and nullifier, proving that the same $\rho^i$ is used in both relations.
5. Verifies knowledge of affirmation secret key by checking:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{pk}} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{pk}} + \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Aff}} \cdot c$$
6. Verifies knowledge of encryption secret key by checking:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{EK}} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{EK}} + \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Enc}} \cdot c$$
7. Verifies that $\mathrm{ek}^{-1}$ is the inverse of $\mathrm{ek}$ by checking:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{G_{\mathrm{Enc}}} \cdot \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Enc}} \stackrel{?}{=} T_{G_{\mathrm{Enc}}} + G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \cdot c$$
   $$\implies (\mathrm{ek}^{-1}_r + \mathrm{ek}^{-1} \cdot c) \cdot \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Enc}} \stackrel{?}{=} \mathrm{ek}^{-1}_r \cdot \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Enc}} + G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \cdot c$$
   Since $\mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Enc}} = \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$, this proves $\mathrm{ek} \cdot \mathrm{ek}^{-1} = 1$.
8. For each leg $k$, verifier checks the participant relation using $\mathrm{resp}_{G_{\mathrm{Enc}}}$:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{G_{\mathrm{Enc}}} \cdot \mathrm{eph\_pk}_{\mathrm{participant},k} \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{participant}_k} + (\mathrm{ct}_{\mathrm{participant},k} - \mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Enc}}) \cdot c$$
9. For each leg $k$ with hidden asset-id, verifier checks asset-id correctness:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{G_{\mathrm{Enc}}} \cdot \mathrm{eph\_pk}_{\mathrm{at},k} \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{at}_k} + (\mathrm{CT}_{\mathrm{at}_k} - \mathrm{at} \cdot H) \cdot c$$
10. If $S$ is non-empty, verifier checks the total sent amount:
    $$\mathrm{resp}_{G_{\mathrm{Enc}}} \cdot (\sum_{k \in S} \mathrm{eph\_pk}_{\mathrm{amt},s_k}) \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{sent}} + (\sum_{k \in S} \mathrm{CT}_{v_k} - \mathrm{pending\_sent} \cdot H) \cdot c$$
11. If $R$ is non-empty, verifier checks the total received amount:
    $$\mathrm{resp}_{G_{\mathrm{Enc}}} \cdot (\sum_{k \in R} \mathrm{eph\_pk}_{\mathrm{amt},r_k}) \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{recv}} + (\sum_{k \in R} \mathrm{CT}_{v_k} - \mathrm{pending\_recv} \cdot H) \cdot c$$


## Transparent transfers

A transparent transfer moves an asset between an on-chain account and an off-chain "transparent" address. Unlike a settlement, it has no receiver affirm — only the 
account holder produces the proof. The asset-id $\mathrm{at}$ and the transfer amount $v$ are both publicly revealed.

There are two variants:

- **Withdraw**: balance decreases by $v$. A range proof on the new balance is enforced to prevent underflow.
- **Deposit**: balance increases by $v$. No range proof is needed — the chain rejects deposits that would overflow $\mathrm{MAX\_BALANCE}$ before the proof is accepted.

In both, $\mathrm{counter}$, $\mathrm{sk}$, $\mathrm{ek}$, $\mathrm{id}$, $\rho$, $s$ and $\mathrm{at}$ are unchanged across the state transition; only $\mathrm{balance}$, $\rho^i$ and $s^i$ change. The account's affirmation public key $\mathrm{AK} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}$ is encrypted for each auditor of the asset using twisted ElGamal so the asset's auditors can identify which account performed the transfer.

Old and new states:
$$
\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{balance} \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{counter} \cdot G_2 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + \rho^i \cdot G_5 + s \cdot G_6 + s^i \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8 + \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}
$$
$$
\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{balance}' \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{counter} \cdot G_2 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + \rho^{i+1} \cdot G_5 + s \cdot G_6 + s^{i+1} \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8 + \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}
$$
with $\mathrm{balance}' = \mathrm{balance} - v$ for withdraw or $\mathrm{balance}' = \mathrm{balance} + v$ for deposit.

### Protocol

_The following description is for W1 (solo) where a single prover knows both secret keys $\mathrm{sk}$ and $\mathrm{ek}$. The split-prover variant used in W2/W3 is described after the Verifier section below._

This section describes a unified protocol that handles both withdraw and deposit. The only differences are the sign of $v$ in the old-state relation and the presence/absence of the new-balance range proof; these are flagged inline.

Here the prover wants to prove following relations:

1. $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{bal}_0 \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{cnt} \cdot G_2 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + \rho^i \cdot G_5 + s \cdot G_6 + s^i \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8 + \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$. Substituting $\mathrm{bal}_0 = \mathrm{bal}_1 \pm v$ (where $-v$ is for withdraw and $+v$ is for deposit) and rearranging:
    - **Withdraw**: $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}} - v \cdot G_1 = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{cnt} \cdot G_2 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + \rho^i \cdot G_5 + s \cdot G_6 + s^i \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8 + \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$
    - **Deposit**: $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}} + v \cdot G_1 = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{cnt} \cdot G_2 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + \rho^i \cdot G_5 + s \cdot G_6 + s^i \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8 + \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$
2. $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{cnt} \cdot G_2 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + \rho^{i+1} \cdot G_5 + s \cdot G_6 + s^{i+1} \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8 + \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$
3. $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}}$ exists in the accumulator (account curve tree)
4. $N = \rho^i \cdot G_5$
5. $\rho^{i+1} = \rho \cdot \rho^i$
6. $s^{i+1} = s \cdot s^i$
7. Withdraw only: $\mathrm{bal}_1 \leq \mathrm{MAX\_BALANCE}$
8. The encrypted public key for the asset's auditors is correctly formed: $\mathrm{encrypted} = r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}$ and for each auditor key $\mathrm{EK}_i$, $\mathrm{eph\_pk}_i = r \cdot \mathrm{EK}_i$. Knowledge of $(r, \mathrm{sk})$ in $\mathrm{encrypted}$ and $r$ in each $\mathrm{eph\_pk}_i$ is proven, with $\mathrm{sk}$ shared with $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}}, \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}$ and the same $r$ shared across all $\mathrm{eph\_pk}_i$.

**Instance**: $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}, \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}, N, \mathrm{Path}_r, \mathrm{Root}, \mathrm{at}, v, \text{direction}, [\mathrm{EK}_i]_{i=1}^n, \mathrm{encrypted}, [\mathrm{eph\_pk}_i]_{i=1}^n, G_{\mathrm{Aff}}, G_{\mathrm{Enc}}, G_i, H_i, \widetilde{G}, \widetilde{G}_i, \widetilde{H}, \widetilde{H}_i$

**Witness**: $\mathrm{sk}, \mathrm{ek}, \mathrm{bal}_0, \mathrm{bal}_1, \mathrm{cnt}, \rho, \rho^i, \rho^{i+1}, s, s^i, s^{i+1}, \mathrm{id}, b_0, r, \mathrm{Path}$

Above are the high level instance and witness values known before the protocol starts and don't include instance and witness created during protocol execution like the various blindings and $T$ values. The variable $r$ is the ephemeral randomness used in the auditor key encryption.

#### Prover

1. Prover gets the path of $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}}$ as $\mathrm{Path}$, randomizes it (blinding $b_0 \cdot H_0$ on the leaf and similar blindings on the path nodes), giving $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r} = \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}} + b_0 \cdot H_0$ and $\mathrm{Path}_r$. Curve tree membership constraints are enforced over $\mathrm{Path}, \mathrm{Path}_r$.
2. Letting $D_{\mathrm{old}}$ denote the public RHS for the old state (which depends on direction):
    - **Withdraw**: $D_{\mathrm{old}} = \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r} - v \cdot G_1 - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3$
    - **Deposit**: $D_{\mathrm{old}} = \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r} + v \cdot G_1 - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3$

    Prover wants to prove knowledge of $\mathrm{sk}, \mathrm{bal}_1, \mathrm{cnt}, \rho, \rho^i, s, s^i, \mathrm{id}, \mathrm{ek}, b_0$ in
    $$\mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{cnt} \cdot G_2 + \rho \cdot G_4 + \rho^i \cdot G_5 + s \cdot G_6 + s^i \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8 + \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + b_0 \cdot H_0 = D_{\mathrm{old}}$$
3. Letting $D_{\mathrm{new}} = \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}} - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3$, prover wants to prove knowledge of $\mathrm{sk}, \mathrm{bal}_1, \mathrm{cnt}, \rho, \rho^{i+1}, s, s^{i+1}, \mathrm{id}, \mathrm{ek}$ in
    $$\mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{cnt} \cdot G_2 + \rho \cdot G_4 + \rho^{i+1} \cdot G_5 + s \cdot G_6 + s^{i+1} \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8 + \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} = D_{\mathrm{new}}$$
4. Prover picks random $\mathrm{sk}_r, \mathrm{ek}_r, \mathrm{bal}_{1_r}, \mathrm{cnt}_r, \rho_r, \rho^i_r, \rho^{i+1}_r, s_r, s^i_r, s^{i+1}_r, \mathrm{id}_r, b_{0_r} \in \mathbb{Z}_p$ and creates:
    $$T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}} = \mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{bal}_{1_r} \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{cnt}_r \cdot G_2 + \rho_r \cdot G_4 + \rho^i_r \cdot G_5 + s_r \cdot G_6 + s^i_r \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{id}_r \cdot G_8 + \mathrm{ek}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + b_{0_r} \cdot H_0$$
    $$T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}} = \mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{bal}_{1_r} \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{cnt}_r \cdot G_2 + \rho_r \cdot G_4 + \rho^{i+1}_r \cdot G_5 + s_r \cdot G_6 + s^{i+1}_r \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{id}_r \cdot G_8 + \mathrm{ek}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$$
    The blindings for $\mathrm{sk}, \mathrm{ek}, \mathrm{bal}_1, \mathrm{cnt}, \rho, s, \mathrm{id}$ are reused between old and new states since these witnesses are the same in both.
5. For the correctness of nullifier, prover creates:
    $$T_{\mathrm{null}} = \rho^i_r \cdot G_5$$
6. For enforcing $\rho^{i+1} = \rho \cdot \rho^i$ and $s^{i+1} = s \cdot s^i$, prover sets up the constraints in Bulletproof. Prover uses Sigma protocol to prove equality of committed values in Bulletproof's commitment and in $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}}, \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}$. The commitment depends on direction (the new balance is appended only when balance decreases, for the range proof):
    - **Withdraw**: $C_{\mathrm{BP}} = b' \cdot H_0 + \rho \cdot H_1 + \rho^i \cdot H_2 + \rho^{i+1} \cdot H_3 + s \cdot H_4 + s^i \cdot H_5 + s^{i+1} \cdot H_6 + \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot H_7$
    - **Deposit**: $C_{\mathrm{BP}} = b' \cdot H_0 + \rho \cdot H_1 + \rho^i \cdot H_2 + \rho^{i+1} \cdot H_3 + s \cdot H_4 + s^i \cdot H_5 + s^{i+1} \cdot H_6$

    For withdraw, an additional Bulletproof range constraint enforces $\mathrm{bal}_1 \leq \mathrm{MAX\_BALANCE}$ on the $\mathrm{bal}_1$ slot.
7. Prover commits to $C_{\mathrm{BP}}$. Pick random $r_{\mathrm{BP}} \in \mathbb{Z}_p$ and create
    $$T_{C_{\mathrm{BP}}} = r_{\mathrm{BP}} \cdot H_0 + \rho_r \cdot H_1 + \rho^i_r \cdot H_2 + \rho^{i+1}_r \cdot H_3 + s_r \cdot H_4 + s^i_r \cdot H_5 + s^{i+1}_r \cdot H_6 \,(+\, \mathrm{bal}_{1_r} \cdot H_7 \text{ for withdraw})$$
8. For the encrypted public key:
    $$\mathrm{encrypted} = r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}$$
    $$\mathrm{eph\_pk}_i = r \cdot \mathrm{EK}_i \quad \text{for each } i = 1, \dots , n$$
    Prover picks random $r_r \in \mathbb{Z}_p$ (reusing $\mathrm{sk}_r$ from step 4) and creates:
    $$T_{\mathrm{encrypted}} = r_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + \mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}$$
    $$T_{\mathrm{eph\_pk}_i} = r_r \cdot \mathrm{EK}_i \quad \text{for each } i = 1, \dots , n$$
9. Prover hashes the following to create challenge $c$ as:
    $$c = \mathrm{Hash}(\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}, \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}, N, \mathrm{Path}_r, \mathrm{Root}, \mathrm{at}, v, \text{direction}, [\mathrm{EK}_i], \mathrm{encrypted}, [\mathrm{eph\_pk}_i], C_{\mathrm{BP}},$$
    $$T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}, T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}}, T_{\mathrm{null}}, T_{C_{\mathrm{BP}}}, T_{\mathrm{encrypted}}, [T_{\mathrm{eph\_pk}_i}])$$
10. Responses for $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}$:
    $$[\begin{matrix}
 \mathrm{sk}_r + \mathrm{sk} \cdot c, & \mathrm{bal}_{1_r} + \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot c, & \mathrm{cnt}_r + \mathrm{cnt} \cdot c, & \rho_r + \rho \cdot c, & \rho^i_r + \rho^i \cdot c, & s_r + s \cdot c, & s^i_r + s^i \cdot c, & \mathrm{id}_r + \mathrm{id} \cdot c, & \mathrm{ek}_r + \mathrm{ek} \cdot c, & b_{0_r} + b_0 \cdot c\\
 \end{matrix}]$$
11. Responses for $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{new}}}$ (most witnesses are shared from $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}$, indicated by $\bot$). Using the same indexing as step 10 (0=$\mathrm{sk}$, 1=$\mathrm{bal}$, 2=$\mathrm{cnt}$, 3=$\rho$, 4=$\rho^i$, 5=$s$, 6=$s^i$, 7=$\mathrm{id}$, 8=$\mathrm{ek}$):
    $$[\begin{matrix}
 \bot, & \bot, & \bot, & \bot, & \rho^{i+1}_r + \rho^{i+1} \cdot c, & \bot, & s^{i+1}_r + s^{i+1} \cdot c, & \bot, & \bot\\
 \end{matrix}]$$
12. For $C_{\mathrm{BP}}$, only the blinding response is created (other witnesses are reused from $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}$ and $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{new}}}$):
    $$\mathrm{resp}_{b'} = r_{\mathrm{BP}} + b' \cdot c$$
13. Response for $r$ (used in encrypted pubkey):
    $$\mathrm{resp}_r = r_r + r \cdot c$$
14. The proof is
    $$(C_{\mathrm{BP}}, \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}, \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}, \mathrm{Path}_r, N, \mathrm{encrypted}, [\mathrm{eph\_pk}_i],$$
    $$T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}, T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}}, T_{\mathrm{null}}, T_{C_{\mathrm{BP}}}, T_{\mathrm{encrypted}}, [T_{\mathrm{eph\_pk}_i}],$$
    $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}, \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{new}}}, \mathrm{resp}_{b'}, \mathrm{resp}_r)$$

#### Verifier

1. Verifier hashes the following to create challenge $c$ as:
    $$c = \mathrm{Hash}(\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}, \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}, N, \mathrm{Path}_r, \mathrm{Root}, \mathrm{at}, v, \text{direction}, [\mathrm{EK}_i], \mathrm{encrypted}, [\mathrm{eph\_pk}_i], C_{\mathrm{BP}},$$
    $$T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}, T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}}, T_{\mathrm{null}}, T_{C_{\mathrm{BP}}}, T_{\mathrm{encrypted}}, [T_{\mathrm{eph\_pk}_i}])$$
2. Verifier enforces the Bulletproof constraints for the 2 multiplications: $\rho^{i+1} = \rho \cdot \rho^i$ and $s^{i+1} = s \cdot s^i$. For withdraw, additionally enforce the range proof $\mathrm{bal}_1 \leq \mathrm{MAX\_BALANCE}$.
3. Verifier enforces the Bulletproof constraints for curve tree membership to verify that $\mathrm{Path}_r$ leads to $\mathrm{Root}$.
4. Let $D_{\mathrm{old}}$ be as defined in Prover step 2 (direction-dependent). Verifies correctness of $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}$ by checking:
    $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[0] \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[1] \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[2] \cdot G_2 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[3] \cdot G_4 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[4] \cdot G_5 + $$
    $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[5] \cdot G_6 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[6] \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[7] \cdot G_8 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[8] \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[9] \cdot H_0 \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}} + D_{\mathrm{old}} \cdot c$$
5. Verifies correctness of $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}$ by checking (reusing responses from $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}$ for shared witnesses):
    $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[0] \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[1] \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[2] \cdot G_2 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[3] \cdot G_4 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{new}}}[4] \cdot G_5 + $$
    $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[5] \cdot G_6 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{new}}}[6] \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[7] \cdot G_8 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[8] \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}} + (\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}} - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3) \cdot c$$
6. Verifies correctness of nullifier by checking:
    $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[4] \cdot G_5 \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{null}} + N \cdot c$$
7. Verifies $C_{\mathrm{BP}}$ (with the $\mathrm{bal}_1$ slot included only for withdraw):
    $$\mathrm{resp}_{b'} \cdot H_0 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[3] \cdot H_1 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[4] \cdot H_2 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{new}}}[4] \cdot H_3 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[5] \cdot H_4 +$$
    $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[6] \cdot H_5 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{new}}}[6] \cdot H_6 \,(+\, \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[1] \cdot H_7 \text{ for withdraw}) \stackrel{?}{=} T_{C_{\mathrm{BP}}} + C_{\mathrm{BP}} \cdot c$$
8. Verifies the encrypted public key:
    $$\mathrm{resp}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[0] \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{encrypted}} + \mathrm{encrypted} \cdot c$$
    (the $\mathrm{sk}$ response is reused from $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[0]$, binding $\mathrm{sk}$ used in the account commitment to the $\mathrm{sk}$ encrypted for auditors).
9. For each auditor key $\mathrm{EK}_i$, verifies the ephemeral key relation:
    $$\mathrm{resp}_r \cdot \mathrm{EK}_i \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{eph\_pk}_i} + \mathrm{eph\_pk}_i \cdot c$$
    The same $\mathrm{resp}_r$ is reused across all $\mathrm{EK}_i$, binding the same $r$ in $\mathrm{encrypted}$ and in every $\mathrm{eph\_pk}_i$.
10. Verifier finally checks the Bulletproofs proof for all constraints i.e. curve tree membership, multiplications, and (for withdraw) the balance range proof.

### Split prover variant (W2/W3)

In W2/W3 the work is split between a host (which does not know $\mathrm{sk}, \mathrm{ek}$) and a device (which holds $\mathrm{sk}, \mathrm{ek}$). Since $\mathrm{AK}$ and $\mathrm{EK}$ are hidden inside the account commitment, the verifier reconstructs $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}$ and $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}$ from device partials and host partials that sum to them (same structure as for affirmations). The encrypted-pubkey-for-auditors part is produced entirely on the device side since it requires $\mathrm{sk}$; there are no leg ciphertexts to split.

#### Account commitment split

Identical to the affirmations split: the device picks fresh independent randomness shares $r_{\mathrm{old},D}, r_{\mathrm{new},D} \in \mathbb{Z}_p$ and publishes
$$P_{\mathrm{old},D} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + r_{\mathrm{old},D} \cdot H_0$$
$$P_{\mathrm{new},D} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + r_{\mathrm{new},D} \cdot H_0$$
The host's complementary partials carry everything else with $r_{\mathrm{old},D} + r_{\mathrm{old},H} = b_0$ and $r_{\mathrm{new},D} + r_{\mathrm{new},H} = 0$.

#### Device prover

1. **Account commitment partials.** Pick fresh blindings $\mathrm{sk}_r, \mathrm{ek}_r, r_{\mathrm{old},D_r}, r_{\mathrm{new},D_r} \in \mathbb{Z}_p$ (same $\mathrm{sk}_r, \mathrm{ek}_r$ for both partials):
    $$T_{P_{\mathrm{old},D}} = \mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{ek}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + r_{\mathrm{old},D_r} \cdot H_0$$
    $$T_{P_{\mathrm{new},D}} = \mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{ek}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + r_{\mathrm{new},D_r} \cdot H_0$$
2. **Encrypted public key for auditors.** Pick fresh $r \in \mathbb{Z}_p$ and compute
    $$\mathrm{encrypted} = r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}$$
    $$\mathrm{eph\_pk}_i = r \cdot \mathrm{EK}_i \quad \text{for each } i = 1, \dots , n$$
    Pick blinding $r_r \in \mathbb{Z}_p$ (reusing $\mathrm{sk}_r$ from step 1) and create:
    $$T_{\mathrm{encrypted}} = r_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + \mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}$$
    $$T_{\mathrm{eph\_pk}_i} = r_r \cdot \mathrm{EK}_i$$
3. Device hashes its transcript (containing $\mathrm{nonce}$, the $\mathrm{txn\_type}$ tag, $P_{\mathrm{old},D}, P_{\mathrm{new},D}, \mathrm{encrypted}, [\mathrm{eph\_pk}_i]$, all $T$ values) to derive challenge $c$.
4. Device responses:
    - $s_{\mathrm{sk}} = \mathrm{sk}_r + \mathrm{sk} \cdot c$ (shared across both account partials and the encrypted-pubkey proof)
    - $s_{\mathrm{ek}} = \mathrm{ek}_r + \mathrm{ek} \cdot c$ (shared across both account partials)
    - $s_{r_{\mathrm{old},D}} = r_{\mathrm{old},D_r} + r_{\mathrm{old},D} \cdot c$, $\quad s_{r_{\mathrm{new},D}} = r_{\mathrm{new},D_r} + r_{\mathrm{new},D} \cdot c$
    - $s_r = r_r + r \cdot c$ (shared across $T_{\mathrm{encrypted}}$ and all $T_{\mathrm{eph\_pk}_i}$)

#### Device verifier

1. Recompute $c$ from the device's transcript.
2. Verify the account partials:
    $$s_{\mathrm{sk}} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + s_{\mathrm{ek}} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + s_{r_{\mathrm{old},D}} \cdot H_0 \stackrel{?}{=} T_{P_{\mathrm{old},D}} + P_{\mathrm{old},D} \cdot c$$
    $$s_{\mathrm{sk}} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + s_{\mathrm{ek}} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + s_{r_{\mathrm{new},D}} \cdot H_0 \stackrel{?}{=} T_{P_{\mathrm{new},D}} + P_{\mathrm{new},D} \cdot c$$
3. Verify the encrypted public key:
    $$s_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + s_{\mathrm{sk}} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{encrypted}} + \mathrm{encrypted} \cdot c$$
4. For each auditor key $\mathrm{EK}_i$, verify the ephemeral key:
    $$s_r \cdot \mathrm{EK}_i \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{eph\_pk}_i} + \mathrm{eph\_pk}_i \cdot c$$

Reusing $s_{\mathrm{sk}}$ across the account partials and the encrypted-pubkey check binds the same $\mathrm{sk}$ across them.

#### Host side (delta from W1)

The host runs the W1 prover with the following changes:

- $\mathrm{sk}, \mathrm{ek}$ are dropped from the witness set. The $\mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{ek}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$ terms are dropped from $T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}$ and $T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}}$, and the corresponding responses are dropped.
- The host's targets are $P_{\mathrm{old},H}$ and $P_{\mathrm{new},H}$ in place of $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}$ and $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}$ (the verifier reconstructs them as $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r} - P_{\mathrm{old},D}$ and $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}} - P_{\mathrm{new},D}$).
- The encrypted-pubkey part of the proof ($T_{\mathrm{encrypted}}, [T_{\mathrm{eph\_pk}_i}], \mathrm{resp}_r$) is dropped — it lives entirely on the device.
- The randomness shares $r_{\mathrm{old},H}, r_{\mathrm{new},H}$ get their own blindings in $T_{P_{\mathrm{old},H}}$ and $T_{P_{\mathrm{new},H}}$ (replacing the $b_0$ blinding in W1's $T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}$).

Nullifier, curve tree membership, $C_{\mathrm{BP}}$ and its multiplication constraints (and the range proof on $\mathrm{bal}_1$ for withdraw) are all unchanged.

#### Verifier additions

In addition to running the device verifier and the host's state/nullifier/BP checks, the verifier:

- Reconstructs $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r} \stackrel{?}{=} P_{\mathrm{old},D} + P_{\mathrm{old},H}$ and $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}} \stackrel{?}{=} P_{\mathrm{new},D} + P_{\mathrm{new},H}$.

#### Binding host and device

**W2 (parallel).** The device's $\mathrm{nonce}$ is the same $\mathrm{nonce}$ the host uses, so both proofs end up bound to the same context. Device and host coordinate on the randomness shares ($r_{\mathrm{old},D}, r_{\mathrm{new},D}$) before proving; the two proofs are then produced and verified independently.

**W3 (sequential).** The host first runs its pre-challenge phase of the host prover above — picking all blindings and computing all host-side $T$ values, with $\mathrm{sk}$- and $\mathrm{ek}$-related parts dropped as described above. It then derives its challenge from that transcript exactly as the W1 challenge $c$ is derived but with the dropped $T$s omitted from the hash; call this challenge $c_H$. The host sends $c_H$ to the device. The device sets its $\mathrm{nonce}$ to
$$\mathrm{nonce}' = c_H \| \mathrm{nonce}$$
(concatenation of the byte serialization of $c_H$ and the original $\mathrm{nonce}$), runs the Device prover above against $\mathrm{nonce}'$, and sends its full proof back to the host. The host appends the device proof bytes to its transcript and derives a fresh challenge
$$c_H' = \mathrm{Hash}(c_H, \text{device proof bytes})$$
which it uses for its own response generation. The verifier mirrors the same ordering: derives $c_H$, reconstructs $\mathrm{nonce}' = c_H \| \mathrm{nonce}$, verifies the device proof against $\mathrm{nonce}'$, then derives $c_H'$ from the device proof bytes and verifies the host's responses against $c_H'$.
