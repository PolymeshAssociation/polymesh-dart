# Registrations

## Key registration
This is the first step where all entities, investors, auditors, mediators, register their keys. This is not registration for an asset but a proof of knowledge of secret key for the public key. A simple implementation of this is a proof of knowledge of the discrete log in these 2 relations which can be done with a folklore sigma protocol (Schnorr):
$$
\mathrm{EK} = \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}, \quad \mathrm{AK} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}
$$

But since we allow users (think institutions) to onboard a large number of keys in a single txn, we use the batch Schnorr protocol from Fig. 2 of [this paper](https://iacr.org/archive/asiacrypt2004/33290273/33290273.pdf) (Fig. 2 has a typo in the last equation where $g^y$ should be $g^s$) and implement the key registration as:

1. Prover (user) wants to register the public keys $[(\mathrm{EK}_1, \mathrm{AK}_1), (\mathrm{EK}_2, \mathrm{AK}_2), \dots (\mathrm{EK}_n, \mathrm{AK}_n)]$ and has the corresponding secret keys $[(\mathrm{ek}_1, \mathrm{sk}_1), (\mathrm{ek}_2, \mathrm{sk}_2), \dots , (\mathrm{ek}_n, \mathrm{sk}_n)]$
2. Prover picks random:

   $$r_{\mathrm{enc}} \stackrel{\$}{\leftarrow} \mathbb{Z}_p$$

   $$r_{\mathrm{aff}} \stackrel{\$}{\leftarrow} \mathbb{Z}_p$$

3. Prover creates $T_{\mathrm{enc}} = r_{\mathrm{enc}} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}, \quad T_{\mathrm{aff}} = r_{\mathrm{aff}} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}$
4. Prover hashes $[(\mathrm{EK}_1, \mathrm{AK}_1), (\mathrm{EK}_2, \mathrm{AK}_2), \dots (\mathrm{EK}_n, \mathrm{AK}_n)]$, $T_{\mathrm{enc}}, T_{\mathrm{aff}}$ to get a challenge $c \in \mathbb{Z}_p$
5. Prover creates responses as:

   $$s_{\mathrm{enc}} = r_{\mathrm{enc}} + \sum_{i=1}^n c^i \cdot \mathrm{ek}_i$$

   $$s_{\mathrm{aff}} = r_{\mathrm{aff}} + \sum_{i=1}^n c^i \cdot \mathrm{sk}_i$$

6. Verifier now checks:

   $$s_{\mathrm{enc}} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{enc}} + \sum_{i=1}^n c^i \cdot \mathrm{EK}_i$$

   $$s_{\mathrm{aff}} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{aff}} + \sum_{i=1}^n c^i \cdot \mathrm{AK}_i$$

Similarly, multiple encryption keys of auditors/mediators can be registered in one key registration proof by following the protocol above and ignoring $\mathrm{AK}_i$ and corresponding relations

## Account registration
This process is used by an investor when it wants to start trading in an asset, i.e. be either a sender or receiver of that asset. It can only be done once by any public key for a particular asset and is done only for non-fee assets. The public key $\mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Aff}}$ must already be registered as per the above protocol
A successful completion of registration results in the following:

1. A state $\mathrm{State}_0$ being inserted as a leaf in the accounts curve tree where
   $$
 \mathrm{State}_0 = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{asset\_id} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + \rho^2 \cdot G_5 + s \cdot G_6 + s^2 \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8 + \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}
 $$
   $\mathrm{balance}$ and $\mathrm{counter}$ are 0 during registration
2. asset-id, public key $(\mathrm{asset\_id}, \mathrm{AK}(=\mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}), \mathrm{EK}(=\mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}))$, are added to the Asset-account mapping
3. Nullifier $N=\rho \cdot G_5$ is revealed by user and chain adds it to the nullifier set.
4. If asset has a trustee with public key $\mathrm{pk}_T$, encryption of $s$ and $\rho$ for $\mathrm{pk}_T$ is published on chain.

**Account creation**

1. User picks a nonce $\mathrm{ctr}$, appends to the asset-id $\mathrm{at}$ to get the combined value $\mathrm{at}\mathbin{\|}\mathrm{ctr}$. It then picks a random $\mathrm{rho\_randomness}$ and passes it along with $\mathrm{at}\mathbin{\|}\mathrm{ctr}$ to Poseidon2 hash to get the nullifier secret key $\rho = \mathrm{Poseidon2}(\mathrm{rho\_randomness}, \mathrm{at}\mathbin{\|}\mathrm{ctr})$. The nonce $\mathrm{ctr}$ is used in case user wants to re-register (account recovery, etc), it can generate a new $\rho$
2. User picks a random:
   
   $$s \stackrel{\$}{\leftarrow} \mathbb{Z}_p$$
   
3. It now creates its initial account state $\mathrm{State}_0 = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{asset\_id} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + \rho^2 \cdot G_5 + s \cdot G_6 + s^2 \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8 + \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$.


### Protocol

_The following description is for W1 (solo) where a single prover knows both secret keys $\mathrm{sk}$ and $\mathrm{ek}$ along with all other witnesses. The split-prover variant used in W2/W3 is described after the Verifier section below._

Here the prover (investor) wants to prove following relations:

1. $\mathrm{State}_0 = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + \rho^2 \cdot G_5 + s \cdot G_6 + s^2 \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8 + \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$. This is equivalent to proving $\mathrm{State}_0 = \mathrm{AK} + \mathrm{EK} + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8 + \rho \cdot G_4 + \rho^2 \cdot G_5 + s \cdot G_6 + s^2 \cdot G_7$ since public key $\mathrm{AK}$, encryption key $\mathrm{EK}$, asset-id $\mathrm{at}$ and identity $\mathrm{id}$ are revealed to the verifier. So only $\rho, \rho^2, s, s^2$ need to be proved.
2. $N = \rho \cdot G_5$
3. $\rho = \mathrm{Poseidon2}(\mathrm{rho\_randomness}, \mathrm{at}\mathbin{\|}\mathrm{ctr})$
4. $\rho^2 = \rho \cdot \rho$
5. $s^2 = s \cdot s$
6. $\mathrm{AK} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}$
7. $\mathrm{EK} = \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$
8. If asset has associated $\mathrm{pk}_T$, then ciphertexts $C_s$ and $C_\rho$ correctly encrypt $s$ and $\rho$ respectively. Since $s, \rho \in \mathbb{Z}_p$ (belong to scalar field of Pallas curve) and are large, 255-bit in our case, if they are just encrypted in exponent-Elgamal, the decryption will be impractical since the decryptor ($\mathrm{pk}_T$) has to solve discrete log of a 255-bit value.
   Thus prover breaks each into small chunks and encrypts each chunk using exponent-Elgamal encryption. These chunks are small enough so that discrete log over them can be completed in reasonable time (few minutes). $\mathrm{CHUNK\_BITS}$ denotes the bit size of each chunk and $\mathrm{NC}$ denotes the total number of chunks. In our implementation, $\mathrm{CHUNK\_BITS}$ = 48 and $\mathrm{NC}$ = 6 since $\lceil {\frac{255}{48}} \rceil = 6$ and this means 6 chunks are sufficient to encode the 255-bit value
   Since Elgamal is homomorphic, these encryptions of chunks can be combined to get an encryption of the whole value which can then be used in a Sigma protocol to show its equality with the value used in account state $\mathrm{State}_0$


Following describes the protocol assuming a $\mathrm{pk}_T$ exists but its simple to omit corresponding parts of the proof if $\mathrm{pk}_T$ does not exist.

**Instance**: $\mathrm{State}_0, \mathrm{AK}, \mathrm{EK}, \mathrm{at}, \mathrm{ctr}, N, \mathrm{id}, C_{s_i}, C_{\rho_i}, \mathrm{pk}_T, G_{\mathrm{Aff}}, G_{\mathrm{Enc}}, G_i, H_i$

**Witness**: $\mathrm{sk}, \mathrm{ek}, \rho, \mathrm{rho\_randomness}, s, s_{\mathrm{chunks}_i}, \rho_{\mathrm{chunks}_i}, r_{\mathrm{enc}_i}, r_{\rho\_\mathrm{enc}_i}$.

Above are the high level instance and witness values known before the protocol starts and don't include instance and witness created during protocol execution like the various blindings and $T$ values.

#### Prover
1. Prover first wants to prove knowledge of $\rho, \rho^2, s, s^2$ in the relation $$\rho \cdot G_4 + \rho^2 \cdot G_5 + s \cdot G_6 + s^2 \cdot G_7 = \mathrm{State}_0 - \mathrm{AK} - \mathrm{EK} - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 - \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8$$. This is just relation 1 from above rearranged where values on RHS are public. Lets call these $D$ as $$D = \mathrm{AK} + \mathrm{EK} + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8$$
   So prover wants to prove knowledge of $\rho, \rho^2, s, s^2$ in $\rho \cdot G_4 + \rho^2 \cdot G_5 + s \cdot G_6 + s^2 \cdot G_7 = \mathrm{State}_0 - D$ and will do with a Sigma protocol
2. Prover picks random $\rho_r, \rho^2_r, s_r, s^2_r \in \mathbb{Z}_p$ and creates
   $$T_{\mathrm{state}} = \rho_r \cdot G_4 + \rho^2_r \cdot G_5 + s_r \cdot G_6 + s^2_r \cdot G_7$$
3. $N = \rho \cdot G_5$ is the initial nullifier. Proves knowledge of $\rho$ in $N$, sharing the blinding $\rho_r$ with the above sigma protocol. Creates:
   $$T_{\mathrm{null}} = \rho_r \cdot G_5$$
4. And to prove knowledge of secret key in its affirmation key $\mathrm{AK}$, it picks random $\mathrm{sk}_r$ and creates $T_{\mathrm{pk}} = \mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}$
5. Prover also wants to prove knowledge of encryption key $\mathrm{ek}$ in $\mathrm{EK}$ as 
   $$\mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} = \mathrm{EK}$$
   It picks random $\mathrm{ek}_r$ and creates 
   $$T_{\mathrm{EK}} = \mathrm{ek}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$$
6. If $\mathrm{pk}_T$ exists, break randomness $s$ in $\mathrm{NC}$ chunks with each chunk as $\mathrm{CHUNK\_BITS}$ bit long and encrypt each using exponent Elgamal as:
   $$
 C_{s_i} = (C_{s_{i,0}}, C_{s_{i,1}}) = (r_{\mathrm{enc}_i} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}, r_{\mathrm{enc}_i} \cdot \mathrm{pk}_T + s_{\mathrm{chunks}_i} \cdot G), \quad C_{s_i} \in \mathbb{G}^2_p
 $$
   where $C_{s_i}$ is ciphertext of $i$-th chunk of $s$, $s_{\mathrm{chunks}}$ is the base-$2^{\mathrm{CHUNK\_BITS}}$ representation of $s$ and it has $\mathrm{NC}$ digits and $r_{\mathrm{enc}_i} \in \mathbb{Z}_p$ is the randomness for the encryption.
   Similarly, break $\rho$ into $\mathrm{NC}$ chunks and encrypt each:
   $$
 C_{\rho_i} = (C_{\rho_{i,0}}, C_{\rho_{i,1}}) = (r_{\rho\_\mathrm{enc}_i} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}, r_{\rho\_\mathrm{enc}_i} \cdot \mathrm{pk}_T + \rho_{\mathrm{chunks}_i} \cdot G), \quad C_{\rho_i} \in \mathbb{G}^2_p
 $$
7. The prover also needs to prove that each chunk (of both $s$ and $\rho$) is a $\mathrm{CHUNK\_BITS}$-bit value to avoid overflow in the base-$2^{\mathrm{CHUNK\_BITS}}$ representation digits (which would make discrete log impractical). For this the prover commits to all chunks of $s$ and $\rho$ in a single Bulletproofs vector commitment $C_{\mathrm{BP\_s}\_\rho}$ and enforces range proof constraints for each chunk.
   $$
 C_{\mathrm{BP\_s}\_\rho} = b' \cdot H_0 + s_{\mathrm{chunks}_0} \cdot H_1 + \dots + s_{\mathrm{chunks}_{\mathrm{NC}-1}} \cdot H_{\mathrm{NC}} + \rho_{\mathrm{chunks}_0} \cdot H_{\mathrm{NC}+1} + \dots + \rho_{\mathrm{chunks}_{\mathrm{NC}-1}} \cdot H_{2 \cdot \mathrm{NC}}, \in \mathbb{G}_p
 $$
   Here $b'$ is blinding to the commitment added by Bulletproofs.
8. Taking a linear combination of the $C_{s_{i,1}}$ components of ciphertexts as below:
   $$\mathrm{Com}_s = \sum_{i} 2^{i \cdot \mathrm{CHUNK\_BITS}} \cdot C_{s_{i,1}} = \sum_{i} 2^{i \cdot \mathrm{CHUNK\_BITS}} \cdot r_{\mathrm{enc}_i} \cdot \mathrm{pk}_T + s \cdot G$$
   The prover uses a Sigma protocol to prove knowledge of the combined randomness $r_{\mathrm{s\_combined}} \in \mathbb{Z}_p$ (where $r_{\mathrm{s\_combined}} = \sum_{i} 2^{i \cdot \mathrm{CHUNK\_BITS}} \cdot r_{\mathrm{enc}_i}$) and $s$ in $\mathrm{Com}_s$. Prover reuses the blinding $s_r$ for $s$ and creates:
   $$T_{s-\mathrm{combined}} = (r_{\mathrm{s\_combined}})_r \cdot \mathrm{pk}_T + s_r \cdot G, \quad T_{s-\mathrm{combined}} \in \mathbb{G}_p$$
   Similarly for $\rho$:
   $$\mathrm{Com}_\rho = \sum_{i} 2^{i \cdot \mathrm{CHUNK\_BITS}} \cdot C_{\rho_{i,1}} = \sum_{i} 2^{i \cdot \mathrm{CHUNK\_BITS}} \cdot r_{\rho\_\mathrm{enc}_i} \cdot \mathrm{pk}_T + \rho \cdot G$$
   $$T_{\rho-\mathrm{combined}} = (r_{\rho\_\mathrm{combined}})_r \cdot \mathrm{pk}_T + \rho_r \cdot G, \quad T_{\rho-\mathrm{combined}} \in \mathbb{G}_p$$
9. For proving knowledge of $r_{\mathrm{enc}_i}, s_{\mathrm{chunks}_i}$ the prover initializes Sigma protocols, 2 for each chunk. It first creates blindings and then commits to them as
   For $i$-th chunk of $s$, pick random $r_{(\mathrm{enc}_i)_r}, s_{(\mathrm{chunks}_i)_r} \in \mathbb{Z}_p$ and create $$T_{\mathrm{enc}_i} = r_{(\mathrm{enc}_i)_r} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}, \quad T_{\mathrm{chunks}_i} = r_{(\mathrm{enc}_i)_r} \cdot \mathrm{pk}_T + s_{(\mathrm{chunks}_i)_r} \cdot G$$, where $T_{\mathrm{enc}_i}, T_{\mathrm{chunks}_i} \in \mathbb{G}_p$.
   Similarly for each chunk of $\rho$, pick random $r_{(\rho\_\mathrm{enc}_i)_r}, \rho_{(\mathrm{chunks}_i)_r} \in \mathbb{Z}_p$ and create corresponding $T$ values.
10. For proving $\rho = \mathrm{Poseidon2}(\mathrm{rho\_randomness}, \mathrm{at}\mathbin{\|}\mathrm{ctr})$, $\rho^2 = \rho \cdot \rho$ and $s^2 = s \cdot s$, prover uses Bulletproofs constraints for Poseidon2 and two multiplications. Let $C_{\mathrm{BP}_\rho}$ be Bulletproof's commitments to these values:
   $$
 C_{\mathrm{BP}_\rho} = b \cdot H_0 + \mathrm{rho\_randomness} \cdot H_1 + \rho \cdot H_2 + \rho^2 \cdot H_3 + s \cdot H_4 + s^2 \cdot H_5, \in \mathbb{G}_p
 $$
   Here $b$ is blinding to the commitment added by Bulletproof.
11. Prover proves knowledge of above committed values and equality of these with other commitments as:
   Pick random $r_{\mathrm{BP}_\rho} \in \mathbb{Z}_p$ and $r_{\mathrm{BP\_s}\_\rho} \in \mathbb{Z}_p$ and create
   
   $$
 T_{\mathrm{BP}_\rho} = r_{\mathrm{BP}_\rho} \cdot H_0 + \mathrm{rho\_randomness}_r \cdot H_1 + \rho_r \cdot H_2 + \rho^2_r \cdot H_3 + s_r \cdot H_4 + s^2_r \cdot H_5, \in \mathbb{G}_p
 $$
   
   $$T_{\mathrm{BP\_s}\_\rho} = r_{\mathrm{BP\_s}\_\rho} \cdot H_0 + s_{(\mathrm{chunks}_0)_r} \cdot H_1 + \dots + s_{(\mathrm{chunks}_{\mathrm{NC}-1})_r} \cdot H_{\mathrm{NC}} + $$
   $$\rho_{(\mathrm{chunks}_0)_r} \cdot H_{\mathrm{NC}+1} + \dots + \rho_{(\mathrm{chunks}_{\mathrm{NC}-1})_r} \cdot H_{2 \cdot \mathrm{NC}}, \in \mathbb{G}_p$$

12. Prover hashes the following to create challenge $c$ as:
    $$c = \mathrm{Hash}(\mathrm{State}_0, \mathrm{AK}, \mathrm{EK}, \mathrm{at}, \mathrm{ctr}, N, \mathrm{id}, C_{s_i}, C_{\rho_i}, \mathrm{pk}_T, \mathrm{Com}_s, \mathrm{Com}_\rho, C_{\mathrm{BP}_\rho}, C_{\mathrm{BP\_s}\_\rho}, $$
    $$T_{\mathrm{state}}, T_{\mathrm{null}}, T_{s-\mathrm{combined}}, T_{\rho-\mathrm{combined}}, T_{\mathrm{enc}_i}, T_{\mathrm{chunks}_i}, T_{\mathrm{BP}_\rho}, T_{\mathrm{BP\_s}\_\rho}, T_{\mathrm{pk}}, T_{\mathrm{EK}})$$
13. Prover now creates responses for each sigma protocol as:
    $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}} = [\begin{matrix}
 \rho_r + \rho \cdot c, & \rho^2_r + \rho^2 \cdot c, & s_r + s \cdot c, & s^2_r + s^2 \cdot c\\
 \end{matrix}]$$
    $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{pk}} = \mathrm{sk}_r + \mathrm{sk} \cdot c$$
    $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{null}} = \rho_r + \rho \cdot c$$
    $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{EK}} = \mathrm{ek}_r + \mathrm{ek} \cdot c$$
14. $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{r\_s\_combined}} = (r_{\mathrm{s\_combined}})_r + r_{\mathrm{s\_combined}} \cdot c$$
    $$\mathrm{resp}_{r\_\rho\_\mathrm{combined}} = (r_{\rho\_\mathrm{combined}})_r + r_{\rho\_\mathrm{combined}} \cdot c$$
    Note that since prover already created response for witness $s$ as $s_r + s \cdot c$ in $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}}$, it has both responses for $\mathrm{Com}_s$. Similarly, the response for $\rho$ is shared with $\mathrm{Com}_\rho$.
15. Creates responses for each chunk ciphertext of $s$:
    $\mathrm{resp}_{s_{i,0}} = r_{(\mathrm{enc}_i)_r} + r_{\mathrm{enc}_i} \cdot c$
    $\mathrm{resp}_{s_{\mathrm{chunks}_i}} = s_{(\mathrm{chunks}_i)_r} + s_{\mathrm{chunks}_i} \cdot c$
    Similarly creates responses for each chunk ciphertext of $\rho$:
    $\mathrm{resp}_{\rho_{i,0}} = r_{(\rho\_\mathrm{enc}_i)_r} + r_{\rho\_\mathrm{enc}_i} \cdot c$
    $\mathrm{resp}_{\rho_{\mathrm{chunks}_i}} = \rho_{(\mathrm{chunks}_i)_r} + \rho_{\mathrm{chunks}_i} \cdot c$
16. For relations, $C_{\mathrm{BP}_\rho}, C_{\mathrm{BP\_s}\_\rho}$, only the responses for witnesses $b, b'$ need to be created as the responses of other witnesses are already created.
    $$\mathrm{resp}_b = r_{\mathrm{BP}_\rho} + b \cdot c$$
    $$\mathrm{resp}_{b'} = r_{\mathrm{BP\_s}\_\rho} + b' \cdot c$$
17. The proof is
    $$(C_{\mathrm{BP}_\rho}, C_{\mathrm{BP\_s}\_\rho},$$
    $$T_{\mathrm{state}}, T_{\mathrm{null}}, T_{\mathrm{BP}_\rho}, T_{\mathrm{BP\_s}\_\rho}, T_{\mathrm{pk}}, T_{\mathrm{EK}},$$
    $$T_{s-\mathrm{combined}}, T_{\rho-\mathrm{combined}}, T_{\mathrm{enc}_i}, T_{\mathrm{chunks}_i},$$
    $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}}, \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{pk}}, \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{null}}, \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{EK}}, \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{r\_s\_combined}}, \mathrm{resp}_{r\_\rho\_\mathrm{combined}}, \mathrm{resp}_{s_{i,0}}, \mathrm{resp}_{s_{\mathrm{chunks}_i}}, \mathrm{resp}_{\rho_{i,0}}, \mathrm{resp}_{\rho_{\mathrm{chunks}_i}}, \mathrm{resp}_b, \mathrm{resp}_{b'})$$
    
#### Verifier

1. Verifier hashes the following to create challenge $c$ as:
   $$c = \mathrm{Hash}(\mathrm{State}_0, \mathrm{AK}, \mathrm{EK}, \mathrm{at}, \mathrm{ctr}, N, \mathrm{id}, C_{s_i}, C_{\rho_i}, \mathrm{pk}_T, \mathrm{Com}_s, \mathrm{Com}_\rho, C_{\mathrm{BP}_\rho}, C_{\mathrm{BP\_s}\_\rho}, $$
   $$T_{\mathrm{state}}, T_{\mathrm{null}}, T_{s-\mathrm{combined}}, T_{\rho-\mathrm{combined}}, T_{\mathrm{enc}_i}, T_{\mathrm{chunks}_i}, T_{\mathrm{BP}_\rho}, T_{\mathrm{BP\_s}\_\rho}, T_{\mathrm{pk}}, T_{\mathrm{EK}})$$
2. Enforces constraints in Bulletproof for Poseidon2 for $\rho$ generation, and multiplications $\rho^2 = \rho \cdot \rho$ and $s^2 = s \cdot s$
3. Enforces constraints in Bulletproof for $2 \cdot \mathrm{NC}$ range proofs, $\mathrm{NC}$ for each of $s$ and $\rho$ chunks.
4. Verifies correctness of $\mathrm{State}_0$ by checking:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}}[0] \cdot G_4 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}}[1] \cdot G_5 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}}[2] \cdot G_6 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}}[3] \cdot G_7 \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{state}} + (\mathrm{State}_0 - D) \cdot c$$
   $$\implies (\rho_r + \rho \cdot c) \cdot G_4 + (\rho^2_r + \rho^2 \cdot c) \cdot G_5 + (s_r + s \cdot c) \cdot G_6 + (s^2_r + s^2 \cdot c) \cdot G_7 \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{state}} + (\mathrm{State}_0 - D) \cdot c$$
5. Verifies correctness of $\mathrm{pk}$ by checking:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{pk}} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}\stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{pk}} + \mathrm{AK} \cdot c$$
   $$\implies (\mathrm{sk}_r + \mathrm{sk} \cdot c) \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} \stackrel{?}{=} \mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{AK} \cdot c$$
6. Verifies correctness of $N$ by checking:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{null}} \cdot G_5 \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{null}} + N \cdot c$$
   $$\implies (\rho_r + \rho \cdot c) \cdot G_5 \stackrel{?}{=} \rho_r \cdot G_5 + N \cdot c$$
7. Verifies encryption key relation:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{EK}} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{EK}} + \mathrm{EK} \cdot c$$
   $$\implies (\mathrm{ek}_r + \mathrm{ek} \cdot c) \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \stackrel{?}{=} \mathrm{ek}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + \mathrm{EK} \cdot c$$
8. Verifier creates $\mathrm{Com}_s$ and $\mathrm{Com}_\rho$ as:
   $$\mathrm{Com}_s = \sum_{i} 2^{i \cdot \mathrm{CHUNK\_BITS}} \cdot C_{s_{i,1}}$$
   $$\mathrm{Com}_\rho = \sum_{i} 2^{i \cdot \mathrm{CHUNK\_BITS}} \cdot C_{\rho_{i,1}}$$
9. Verifies correctness of $\mathrm{Com}_s$ by checking:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{r\_s\_combined}} \cdot \mathrm{pk}_T + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}}[2] \cdot G \stackrel{?}{=} T_{s-\mathrm{combined}} + \mathrm{Com}_s \cdot c$$
   Similarly for $\mathrm{Com}_\rho$:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{r\_\rho\_\mathrm{combined}} \cdot \mathrm{pk}_T + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}}[0] \cdot G \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\rho-\mathrm{combined}} + \mathrm{Com}_\rho \cdot c$$
10. Verifier checks response for each $s$ chunk ciphertext $C_{s_{i,0}}$:
    $$\mathrm{resp}_{s_{i,0}} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{enc}_i} + C_{s_{i,0}} \cdot c$$
11. Verifier checks response for each $s$ chunk ciphertext $C_{s_{i,1}}$:
    $$\mathrm{resp}_{s_{i,0}} \cdot \mathrm{pk}_T + \mathrm{resp}_{s_{\mathrm{chunks}_i}} \cdot G \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{chunks}_i} + C_{s_{i,1}} \cdot c$$
    Analogous checks are done for each $\rho$ chunk ciphertext.
12. Verifier checks response for $C_{\mathrm{BP}_\rho}$ as:
    $$\mathrm{resp}_b \cdot H_0 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}}[0] \cdot H_2 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}}[1] \cdot H_3 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}}[2] \cdot H_4 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}}[3] \cdot H_5 \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{BP}_\rho} + C_{\mathrm{BP}_\rho} \cdot c$$
    Note: responses for $\mathrm{rho\_randomness}$ are provided as part of $\mathrm{resp}_b$ (the partial response), while the responses for $\rho, \rho^2, s, s^2$ are reused from $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}}$.
13. Verifier checks response for $C_{\mathrm{BP\_s}\_\rho}$ as:
    $$\mathrm{resp}_{b'} \cdot H_0 + \mathrm{resp}_{s_{\mathrm{chunks}_0}} \cdot H_1 + \dots + \mathrm{resp}_{s_{\mathrm{chunks}_{\mathrm{NC}-1}}} \cdot H_{\mathrm{NC}} + \mathrm{resp}_{\rho_{\mathrm{chunks}_0}} \cdot H_{\mathrm{NC}+1} + \dots + \mathrm{resp}_{\rho_{\mathrm{chunks}_{\mathrm{NC}-1}}} \cdot H_{2 \cdot \mathrm{NC}} \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{BP\_s}\_\rho} + C_{\mathrm{BP\_s}\_\rho} \cdot c$$
14. Verifier finally checks the Bulletproofs proof that verifies all constraints, i.e for Poseidon2, multiplications and range proofs


### Split prover variant (W2/W3)

In W2/W3 the work is split between a host (which does not know $\mathrm{sk}, \mathrm{ek}$) and a device (which holds $\mathrm{sk}, \mathrm{ek}$). The host runs essentially the same protocol as above except its statement excludes the secret-key components: since $\mathrm{AK}$ and $\mathrm{EK}$ are public, the verifier can subtract them out, so the host only proves knowledge of the remaining witnesses in
$$
\mathrm{State}_{0_H} = \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + \rho^2 \cdot G_5 + s \cdot G_6 + s^2 \cdot G_7 + \mathrm{id} \cdot G_8
$$
i.e. $\mathrm{State}_0$ minus $\mathrm{AK}$ and $\mathrm{EK}$. Everything else the host does (nullifier proof, encryption-of-$s$ and $\rho$ to $\mathrm{pk}_T$, range proofs, Poseidon2 constraint, etc.) is unchanged. The verifier reconstructs $\mathrm{State}_0 = \mathrm{State}_{0_H} + \mathrm{AK} + \mathrm{EK}$ and uses it in the original checks.

The device produces a standalone Sigma proof of knowledge of $\mathrm{sk}$ in $\mathrm{AK} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}$ and $\mathrm{ek}$ in $\mathrm{EK} = \mathrm{ek} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$. Both relations share the same challenge (single transcript).

**Instance**: $\mathrm{AK}, \mathrm{EK}, G_{\mathrm{Aff}}, G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$

**Witness**: $\mathrm{sk}, \mathrm{ek}$

#### Device prover
1. Pick random $\mathrm{sk}_r, \mathrm{ek}_r \in \mathbb{Z}_p$ and compute
   $$T_{\mathrm{AK}} = \mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}, \quad T_{\mathrm{EK}} = \mathrm{ek}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}}$$
2. Hash the device's transcript to get a challenge
   $$c = \mathrm{Hash}(\mathrm{nonce}, \mathrm{AK}, \mathrm{EK}, T_{\mathrm{AK}}, T_{\mathrm{EK}})$$
3. Responses
   $$s_{\mathrm{sk}} = \mathrm{sk}_r + \mathrm{sk} \cdot c, \quad s_{\mathrm{ek}} = \mathrm{ek}_r + \mathrm{ek} \cdot c$$

The device sends $(T_{\mathrm{AK}}, T_{\mathrm{EK}}, s_{\mathrm{sk}}, s_{\mathrm{ek}})$ to the verifier (or, in W3, to the host first).

#### Device verifier
1. Recompute the challenge:
   $$c = \mathrm{Hash}(\mathrm{nonce}, \mathrm{AK}, \mathrm{EK}, T_{\mathrm{AK}}, T_{\mathrm{EK}})$$
2. Verify the affirmation key relation:
   $$s_{\mathrm{sk}} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{AK}} + \mathrm{AK} \cdot c$$
   $$\implies (\mathrm{sk}_r + \mathrm{sk} \cdot c) \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} \stackrel{?}{=} \mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{AK} \cdot c$$
3. Verify the encryption key relation:
   $$s_{\mathrm{ek}} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{EK}} + \mathrm{EK} \cdot c$$
   $$\implies (\mathrm{ek}_r + \mathrm{ek} \cdot c) \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \stackrel{?}{=} \mathrm{ek}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + \mathrm{EK} \cdot c$$

#### Binding host and device

**W2 (parallel).** The device's $\mathrm{nonce}$ is the same $\mathrm{nonce}$ the host uses, so both proofs end up bound to the same context. The two proofs are produced independently and verified independently; nothing flows from one prover to the other during proving.

**W3 (sequential).** The host first runs its pre-challenge phase of the Prover above — picking all blindings and computing all $T$ values on the host side, but dropping the parts that involve $\mathrm{sk}$ or $\mathrm{ek}$ (so $T_{\mathrm{pk}}$ and $T_{\mathrm{EK}}$ are not computed and not added to its transcript). It then derives its challenge from that transcript exactly as the W1 challenge $c$ is derived, but with $T_{\mathrm{pk}}$ and $T_{\mathrm{EK}}$ omitted from the hash; call this challenge $c_H$. The host sends $c_H$ to the device. The device sets its $\mathrm{nonce}$ to
$$\mathrm{nonce}' = c_H \| \mathrm{nonce}$$
(concatenation of the byte serialization of $c_H$ and the original $\mathrm{nonce}$), runs the Device prover above against $\mathrm{nonce}'$ — so its challenge becomes
$$c = \mathrm{Hash}(\mathrm{nonce}', \mathrm{AK}, \mathrm{EK}, T_{\mathrm{AK}}, T_{\mathrm{EK}}) = \mathrm{Hash}((c_H \| \mathrm{nonce}), \mathrm{AK}, \mathrm{EK}, T_{\mathrm{AK}}, T_{\mathrm{EK}})$$
— and sends $(T_{\mathrm{AK}}, T_{\mathrm{EK}}, s_{\mathrm{sk}}, s_{\mathrm{ek}})$ back to the host. The host appends the device proof bytes to its transcript and derives a fresh challenge
$$c_H' = \mathrm{Hash}(c_H, T_{\mathrm{AK}}, T_{\mathrm{EK}}, s_{\mathrm{sk}}, s_{\mathrm{ek}})$$
which it uses for its own response generation. The verifier mirrors the same ordering: derives $c_H$, reconstructs $\mathrm{nonce}' = c_H \| \mathrm{nonce}$, verifies the device proof against $\mathrm{nonce}'$, then derives $c_H'$ from the device proof bytes and verifies the host's responses against $c_H'$.


## Encryption key distribution
When an asset has a shared encryption key, the asset issuer distributes the corresponding secret key $\mathrm{ek}$ to the relevant auditors/mediators using Twisted Elgamal and a key-distribution proof which proves to the chain that issuer has indeed encrypted the secret key for the intended recipients and they can decrypt to get it. The issuer splits $\mathrm{ek}$ into chunks $\mathrm{ek}_i$ and for each chunk produces:
- A shared ciphertext: $C_i = G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \cdot r_i + H \cdot \mathrm{ek}_i$
- A per-recipient ciphertext: $E_{ji} = \mathrm{EK}_j \cdot r_i$ for each recipient $j$ with encryption public key $\mathrm{EK}_j$

Each recipient $j$ decrypts its chunk as $\mathrm{ek}_i = C_i - \mathrm{ek}_j^{-1} \cdot E_{ji}$ and recovers the discrete log of $C_i - E_{ji} \cdot \mathrm{ek}_j^{-1}$ over $H$. The proof proves all chunks are in range, all randomnesses are consistent (same $r_i$ used in shared and per-recipient ciphertexts), and the chunks reconstruct the full $\mathrm{ek}$. Twisted Elgamal is used to do the range proof on each chunk only once and not per recipient.

### Protocol
Here the prover (issuer) wants to prove following relations:

1. $C_i = G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \cdot r_i + H \cdot \mathrm{ek}_i$ for each chunk $i$ (correct shared ciphertext)
2. $E_{ji} = \mathrm{EK}_j \cdot r_i$ for each chunk $i$ and recipient $j$ (correct per-recipient ciphertext, using same $r_i$)
3. $\mathrm{pk} = G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \cdot \mathrm{ek}$
4. $\mathrm{ek}_i \in [0, 2^{\mathrm{CHUNK\_BITS}})$ for each chunk $i$ (range correctness)
5. The chunks $\mathrm{ek}_i$ reconstruct to $\mathrm{ek}$ committed in $\mathrm{pk}$: $\sum_i 2^{i \cdot \mathrm{CHUNK\_BITS}} \cdot \mathrm{ek}_i = \mathrm{ek}$

**Instance**: $\mathrm{pk}~(= G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \cdot \mathrm{ek}), \mathrm{EK}_1, \dots , \mathrm{EK}_n, G_{\mathrm{Enc}}, H$

**Witness**: $\mathrm{ek}, \mathrm{ek}_0, \dots , \mathrm{ek}_{\mathrm{NC}-1}, r_0, \dots , r_{\mathrm{NC}-1}$

Above are the high level instance and witness values. The secret key satisfies $\mathrm{ek} = \sum_{i} 2^{i \cdot \mathrm{CHUNK\_BITS}} \cdot \mathrm{ek}_i$.

#### Prover
1. Split $\mathrm{ek}$ into $\mathrm{NC}$ chunks of $\mathrm{CHUNK\_BITS}$ bits each: $\mathrm{ek} = \sum_{i} 2^{i \cdot \mathrm{CHUNK\_BITS}} \cdot \mathrm{ek}_i$.

2. For each chunk $i$, pick random $r_i \in \mathbb{Z}_p$ and compute:
   - Shared ciphertext: $C_i = G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \cdot r_i + H \cdot \mathrm{ek}_i$
   - Per-recipient ciphertexts: $E_{ji} = \mathrm{EK}_j \cdot r_i$ for each recipient $j$

3. For each chunk $i$, to prove $C_i = G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \cdot r_i + H \cdot \mathrm{ek}_i$, pick random $r_{i,r}, \mathrm{ek}_{i,r} \in \mathbb{Z}_p$ and create:
   $$T_i = G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \cdot r_{i,r} + H \cdot \mathrm{ek}_{i,r}$$
   Note: The response for $\mathrm{ek}_i$ will be shared with the Bulletproof vector commitment Schnorr protocol (step 6).

4. For each chunk $i$ and recipient $j$, to prove $E_{ji} = \mathrm{EK}_j \cdot r_i$, reuse the same blinding $r_{i,r}$ from step 3 and create:
   $$T_{ji} = \mathrm{EK}_j \cdot r_{i,r}$$
   Sharing $r_{i,r}$ cryptographically binds the same $r_i$ in both the shared and per-recipient ciphertexts.

5. Compute the combined randomness $r_{\mathrm{combined}} = \sum_{i} 2^{i \cdot \mathrm{CHUNK\_BITS}} \cdot r_i$ and the combined ciphertext:
   $$\mathrm{Com}_{\mathrm{ek}} = G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \cdot r_{\mathrm{combined}} + H \cdot \mathrm{ek} = \sum_{i} 2^{i \cdot \mathrm{CHUNK\_BITS}} \cdot C_i$$
   To prove knowledge of $r_{\mathrm{combined}}$ and $\mathrm{ek}$ in $\mathrm{Com}_{\mathrm{ek}}$, pick random $r_{\mathrm{combined},r}, \mathrm{ek}_r \in \mathbb{Z}_p$ and create:
   $$T_{\mathrm{combined}} = G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \cdot r_{\mathrm{combined},r} + H \cdot \mathrm{ek}_r$$

6. To prove $\mathrm{pk} = G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \cdot \mathrm{ek}$, reuse blinding $\mathrm{ek}_r$ from step 5 and create:
   $$T_{\mathrm{pk}} = G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \cdot \mathrm{ek}_r$$
   Note: $\mathrm{ek}_r$ is reused for proving $\mathrm{pk}$ since the witness is the same $\mathrm{ek}$.

7. Prover also needs to prove that each chunk $\mathrm{ek}_i$ is a $\mathrm{CHUNK\_BITS}$-bit value to avoid overflow in the base-$2^{\mathrm{CHUNK\_BITS}}$ representation. For this it commits to all chunks in a Bulletproofs vector commitment and enforces range proof constraints for each chunk $\mathrm{ek}_i$:
   $$C_{\mathrm{BP}} = b \cdot H_0 + \mathrm{ek}_0 \cdot H_1 + \mathrm{ek}_1 \cdot H_2 + \dots + \mathrm{ek}_{\mathrm{NC}-1} \cdot H_{\mathrm{NC}} \in \mathbb{G}$$
   Here $b$ is the blinding added by Bulletproofs.

8. To bind the BP commitment to the sigma protocols, pick random $b_r \in \mathbb{Z}_p$ and create the Schnorr commitment, reusing $\mathrm{ek}_{i,r}$ from step 3 for each chunk $i$:
   $$T_{\mathrm{BP}} = b_r \cdot H_0 + \mathrm{ek}_{0,r} \cdot H_1 + \mathrm{ek}_{1,r} \cdot H_2 + \dots + \mathrm{ek}_{(\mathrm{NC}-1),r} \cdot H_{\mathrm{NC}}$$
   Sharing $\mathrm{ek}_{i,r}$ ties the $\mathrm{ek}_i$ committed in $C_{\mathrm{BP}}$ to the $\mathrm{ek}_i$ used in the shared ciphertext $C_i$.

9. Prover hashes the following to create challenge $c$:
   $$c = \mathrm{Hash}(\text{nonce},~\mathrm{pk},~\mathrm{EK}_1, \dots , \mathrm{EK}_n,~C_0, \dots , C_{\mathrm{NC}-1},~T_0, \dots , T_{\mathrm{NC}-1},$$
   $$E_{10}, \dots , E_{n,\mathrm{NC}-1},~T_{10}, \dots , T_{n,\mathrm{NC}-1},$$
   $$\mathrm{Com}_{\mathrm{ek}},~T_{\mathrm{combined}},~T_{\mathrm{pk}},~C_{\mathrm{BP}},~T_{\mathrm{BP}})$$

10. Prover now creates responses:
    $$\mathrm{resp}_{r_i} = r_{i,r} + r_i \cdot c \quad \text{(for each chunk } i\text{)}$$
    Note that $\mathrm{resp}_{r_i}$ is reused for proving each $E_{ji}$ (step 4) since the witness is the same $r_i$.

    Responses $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{ek}_i}$ for each chunk come from the Bulletproof Schnorr protocol:
    $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{BP}} = [b_r + b \cdot c,\ \ \mathrm{ek}_{0,r} + \mathrm{ek}_0 \cdot c,\ \ \mathrm{ek}_{1,r} + \mathrm{ek}_1 \cdot c,\ \ \dots ,\ \ \mathrm{ek}_{(\mathrm{NC}-1),r} + \mathrm{ek}_{\mathrm{NC}-1} \cdot c]$$

    $$\mathrm{resp}_{r_{\mathrm{combined}}} = r_{\mathrm{combined},r} + r_{\mathrm{combined}} \cdot c$$
    $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{ek}} = \mathrm{ek}_r + \mathrm{ek} \cdot c$$
    Note that $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{ek}}$ is reused for proving $\mathrm{pk}$ (step 6) since the witness is the same $\mathrm{ek}$.

#### Verifier

1. Verifier hashes the following to create challenge $c$:
   $$c = \mathrm{Hash}(\text{nonce},~\mathrm{pk},~\mathrm{EK}_1, \dots , \mathrm{EK}_n,~C_0, \dots , C_{\mathrm{NC}-1},~T_0, \dots , T_{\mathrm{NC}-1},$$
   $$E_{10}, \dots , E_{n,\mathrm{NC}-1},~T_{10}, \dots , T_{n,\mathrm{NC}-1},$$
   $$\mathrm{Com}_{\mathrm{ek}},~T_{\mathrm{combined}},~T_{\mathrm{pk}},~C_{\mathrm{BP}},~T_{\mathrm{BP}})$$

2. Enforces range proof constraints in Bulletproofs for each chunk $\mathrm{ek}_i$ to be in $[0, 2^{\mathrm{CHUNK\_BITS}})$.

3. For each chunk $i$, verifies the shared ciphertext using $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{BP}}[i+1]$ as $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{ek}_i}$:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{r_i} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{ek}_i} \cdot H \stackrel{?}{=} T_i + C_i \cdot c$$
   $$\implies (r_{i,r} + r_i \cdot c) \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + (\mathrm{ek}_{i,r} + \mathrm{ek}_i \cdot c) \cdot H \stackrel{?}{=} G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \cdot r_{i,r} + H \cdot \mathrm{ek}_{i,r} + C_i \cdot c$$

4. For each chunk $i$ and recipient $j$, verifies the per-recipient ciphertext using $\mathrm{resp}_{r_i}$ from step 3:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{r_i} \cdot \mathrm{EK}_j \stackrel{?}{=} T_{ji} + E_{ji} \cdot c$$
   $$\implies (r_{i,r} + r_i \cdot c) \cdot \mathrm{EK}_j \stackrel{?}{=} \mathrm{EK}_j \cdot r_{i,r} + E_{ji} \cdot c$$

5. Verifies the Bulletproof Schnorr commitment (binding $C_{\mathrm{BP}}$ to the sigma protocols):
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{BP}}[0] \cdot H_0 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{ek}_0} \cdot H_1 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{ek}_1} \cdot H_2 + \dots + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{ek}_{\mathrm{NC}-1}} \cdot H_{\mathrm{NC}} \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{BP}} + C_{\mathrm{BP}} \cdot c$$
   $$\implies (b_r + b \cdot c) \cdot H_0 + (\mathrm{ek}_{0,r} + \mathrm{ek}_0 \cdot c) \cdot H_1 + \dots \stackrel{?}{=} b_r \cdot H_0 + \mathrm{ek}_{0,r} \cdot H_1 + \dots + C_{\mathrm{BP}} \cdot c$$

6. Computes the combined ciphertext:
   $$\mathrm{Com}_{\mathrm{ek}} = \sum_{i} 2^{i \cdot \mathrm{CHUNK\_BITS}} \cdot C_i$$

7. Verifies the combined ciphertext:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{r_{\mathrm{combined}}} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{ek}} \cdot H \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{combined}} + \mathrm{Com}_{\mathrm{ek}} \cdot c$$
   $$\implies (r_{\mathrm{combined},r} + r_{\mathrm{combined}} \cdot c) \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} + (\mathrm{ek}_r + \mathrm{ek} \cdot c) \cdot H \stackrel{?}{=} G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \cdot r_{\mathrm{combined},r} + H \cdot \mathrm{ek}_r + \mathrm{Com}_{\mathrm{ek}} \cdot c$$

8. Verifies the public key using $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{ek}}$ from step 7:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{ek}} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{pk}} + \mathrm{pk} \cdot c$$
   $$\implies (\mathrm{ek}_r + \mathrm{ek} \cdot c) \cdot G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \stackrel{?}{=} G_{\mathrm{Enc}} \cdot \mathrm{ek}_r + \mathrm{pk} \cdot c$$

9. Verifier finally checks the Bulletproofs proof that verifies all range constraints.
