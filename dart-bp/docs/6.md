# Fee

Fee accounts are different from regular accounts and their states are tracked in a different curve tree. Fee accounts also transition from one state to another, invalidating old state by revealing the nullifier. 
Fee can be paid in designated assets and these are different from non-fee paying assets. These assets don't have auditors or mediators. Fee accounts support minting and spend. During both the amount minted or spent 
is public but the balance of new state is kept private. Also, for fee payment asset-id is always revealed. Nullifier secret key $\rho$ and commitment randomness $s$ are not generated deterministically but chosen randomly. 
They don't have an id or counter. 

Following shows a state transition in fee account.

$$
\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{balance} \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + s \cdot G_6, \quad \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}} \in \mathbb{G}_p
$$

$$
\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{balance}' \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho' \cdot G_4 + s' \cdot G_6, \quad \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}} \in \mathbb{G}_p
$$


## Fee account registration
A user can register fee account with a non-zero balance. The asset-id, balance and public key are public during registration.
A successful completion of registration results in the following:

1. A state $\mathrm{State}_0$ being inserted as a leaf in the accounts curve tree where
   $$
 \mathrm{State}_0 = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{balance} \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + s \cdot G_6
 $$
2. Pair asset-id, public key $(\mathrm{asset\_id}$, $\mathrm{PK}_{\mathrm{Aff}}(=\mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}))$ is added to the Asset-account mapping

### Protocol

_The following description is for W1 (solo) where a single prover knows the secret key $\mathrm{sk}$ along with all other witnesses. The split-prover variant used in W2/W3 is described after the Verifier section below._

Here the prover (investor) wants to prove following relations:

1. $$\mathrm{State}_0 = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{balance} \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + s \cdot G_6$$. This is equivalent to proving $\mathrm{State}_0 - \mathrm{AK} - \mathrm{balance} \cdot G_1 - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 = \rho \cdot G_4 + s \cdot G_6$ since $\mathrm{AK}, \mathrm{balance}, \mathrm{at}$ are revealed to the verifier.
2. $\mathrm{AK} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}$

#### Prover

1. Prover wants to prove knowledge of $\rho, s$ in the relation $\rho \cdot G_4 + s \cdot G_6 = \mathrm{State}_0 - \mathrm{AK} - \mathrm{balance} \cdot G_1 - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3$ where the RHS is public.
2. Similarly, prover wants to prove knowledge of $\mathrm{sk}$ in $\mathrm{AK} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}$.
3. Prover picks random:
   
   $$\mathrm{sk}_r, \rho_r, s_r \stackrel{\$}{\leftarrow} \mathbb{Z}_p$$
   
   and creates:
   $$T_{\mathrm{state}} = \rho_r \cdot G_4 + s_r \cdot G_6, \in \mathbb{G}_p$$
4. For proving knowledge of secret key, prover creates:
   $$T_{\mathrm{pk}} = \mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}, \in \mathbb{G}_p$$
5. Prover hashes the following to create challenge $c$ as:
   $$c = \mathrm{Hash}(\mathrm{State}_0, \mathrm{AK}, \mathrm{balance}, \mathrm{at}, T_{\mathrm{state}}, T_{\mathrm{pk}})$$
6. Prover creates responses for $\mathrm{Resp}_{\mathrm{state}}$:
   $$\mathrm{Resp}_{\mathrm{state}} = [\begin{matrix}
 \rho_r + \rho \cdot c, & s_r + s \cdot c\\
 \end{matrix}]$$
7. Response for secret key:
   $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{pk}} = \mathrm{sk}_r + \mathrm{sk} \cdot c$
8. The proof is
   $$
 (\mathrm{State}_0, \mathrm{AK}, \mathrm{balance}, \mathrm{at}, T_{\mathrm{state}}, T_{\mathrm{pk}}, \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}}, \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{pk}})
 $$


#### Verifier

1. Verifier hashes the following to create challenge $c$ as:
   $$c = \mathrm{Hash}(\mathrm{State}_0, \mathrm{AK}, \mathrm{balance}, \mathrm{at}, T_{\mathrm{state}}, T_{\mathrm{pk}})$$
2. Verifies correctness of $\mathrm{State}_0$ by checking:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}}[0] \cdot G_4 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}}[1] \cdot G_6 \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{state}} + (\mathrm{State}_0 - \mathrm{AK} - \mathrm{balance} \cdot G_1 - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3) \cdot c$$

   $$\implies (\rho_r + \rho \cdot c) \cdot G_4 + (s_r + s \cdot c) \cdot G_6 \stackrel{?}{=} \rho_r \cdot G_4 + s_r \cdot G_6 + (\mathrm{State}_0 - \mathrm{AK} - \mathrm{balance} \cdot G_1 - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3) \cdot c$$

3. Verifies knowledge of secret key by checking:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{pk}} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{pk}} + \mathrm{AK} \cdot c$$
   $$\implies (\mathrm{sk}_r + \mathrm{sk} \cdot c) \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} \stackrel{?}{=} \mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{AK} \cdot c$$


### Split prover variant (W2/W3)

In W2/W3 the work is split between a host (which does not know $\mathrm{sk}$) and a device (which holds $\mathrm{sk}$). Since $\mathrm{AK}$ is revealed during fee account registration, the host's statement excludes the secret-key component:
$$
\mathrm{State}_{0_H} = \mathrm{balance} \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + s \cdot G_6
$$
i.e. $\mathrm{State}_0$ minus $\mathrm{AK}$. Everything else the host does is unchanged. The verifier reconstructs $\mathrm{State}_0 = \mathrm{State}_{0_H} + \mathrm{AK}$ and uses it in the original check.

The device produces a standalone Sigma proof of knowledge of $\mathrm{sk}$ in $\mathrm{AK} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}$.

**Instance**: $\mathrm{AK}, G_{\mathrm{Aff}}$

**Witness**: $\mathrm{sk}$

#### Device prover
1. Pick random $\mathrm{sk}_r \in \mathbb{Z}_p$ and compute
   $$T_{\mathrm{AK}} = \mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}$$
2. Hash the device's transcript to get a challenge
   $$c = \mathrm{Hash}(\mathrm{nonce}, \mathrm{AK}, T_{\mathrm{AK}})$$
3. Response
   $$s_{\mathrm{sk}} = \mathrm{sk}_r + \mathrm{sk} \cdot c$$

The device sends $(T_{\mathrm{AK}}, s_{\mathrm{sk}})$ to the verifier (or, in W3, to the host first).

#### Device verifier
1. Recompute the challenge:
   $$c = \mathrm{Hash}(\mathrm{nonce}, \mathrm{AK}, T_{\mathrm{AK}})$$
2. Verify the affirmation key relation:
   $$s_{\mathrm{sk}} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{AK}} + \mathrm{AK} \cdot c$$
   $$\implies (\mathrm{sk}_r + \mathrm{sk} \cdot c) \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} \stackrel{?}{=} \mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{AK} \cdot c$$

#### Binding host and device

**W2 (parallel).** The device's $\mathrm{nonce}$ is the same $\mathrm{nonce}$ the host uses, so both proofs end up bound to the same context. The two proofs are produced independently and verified independently; nothing flows from one prover to the other during proving.

**W3 (sequential).** The host first runs its pre-challenge phase of the Prover above — picking all blindings and computing all $T$ values on the host side, but dropping the parts that involve $\mathrm{sk}$ (so $T_{\mathrm{pk}}$ is not computed and not added to its transcript). It then derives its challenge from that transcript exactly as the W1 challenge $c$ is derived, but with $T_{\mathrm{pk}}$ omitted from the hash; call this challenge $c_H$. The host sends $c_H$ to the device. The device sets its $\mathrm{nonce}$ to
$$\mathrm{nonce}' = c_H \| \mathrm{nonce}$$
(concatenation of the byte serialization of $c_H$ and the original $\mathrm{nonce}$), runs the Device prover above against $\mathrm{nonce}'$ — so its challenge becomes
$$c = \mathrm{Hash}(\mathrm{nonce}', \mathrm{AK}, T_{\mathrm{AK}}) = \mathrm{Hash}((c_H \| \mathrm{nonce}), \mathrm{AK}, T_{\mathrm{AK}})$$
— and sends $(T_{\mathrm{AK}}, s_{\mathrm{sk}})$ back to the host. The host appends the device proof bytes to its transcript and derives a fresh challenge
$$c_H' = \mathrm{Hash}(c_H, T_{\mathrm{AK}}, s_{\mathrm{sk}})$$
which it uses for its own response generation. The verifier mirrors the same ordering: derives $c_H$, reconstructs $\mathrm{nonce}' = c_H \| \mathrm{nonce}$, verifies the device proof against $\mathrm{nonce}'$, then derives $c_H'$ from the device proof bytes and verifies the host's responses against $c_H'$.


## Account top-up
This is similar to regular account's minting txn where the balance in $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}$ is increased by a public amount $v$ and the public key $\mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}$ is revealed as well.

A successful completion of top-up txn results in the following:

1. The user's old account state $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}}$ is invalidated and new state $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}$ is created. However, $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}}$ cannot be revealed as it identifies which account state is being invalidated. So a randomized version of it $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}$ is used in relations.
2. Nullifier $N=\rho \cdot G_4$ is revealed by issuer and chain adds it to the nullifier set. Here $\rho$ refers to the nullifier secret key of $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}}$
3. The balance in $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}$ is more than balance in $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}}$ by the minted amount
4. $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}$ is inserted as a new leaf in the curve tree.

### Protocol

_The following description is for W1 (solo) where a single prover knows the secret key $\mathrm{sk}$ along with all other witnesses. The split-prover variant used in W2/W3 is described after the Verifier section below._

Here the prover (user) wants to prove following relations when minting amount $v$:

1. $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{bal}_0 \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + s \cdot G_6$. This is equivalent to proving $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}} = \mathrm{AK} + \mathrm{bal}_0 \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + s \cdot G_6$ since public key $\mathrm{AK}$ is revealed to the verifier. Since $\mathrm{bal}_0 + v = \mathrm{bal}_1$, this is equivalent to $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}} + v \cdot G_1 = \mathrm{AK} + \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + s \cdot G_6$
2. Similarly, $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}} = \mathrm{AK} + \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho' \cdot G_4 + s' \cdot G_6$.
3. $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}}$ exists in the accumulator (account curve tree)
4. $N = \rho \cdot G_4$
5. $\mathrm{AK} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}$
6. $\mathrm{bal}_1 = \mathrm{bal}_0 + v$
7. $\mathrm{bal}_1 \leq \mathrm{MAX\_FEE\_BALANCE}$

**Instance**: $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}, \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}, \mathrm{AK}, \mathrm{at}, N, v, \mathrm{Path}_r, \mathrm{Root}, G_{\mathrm{Aff}}, G_i, H_i$

**Witness**: $\mathrm{sk}, \mathrm{bal}_0, \mathrm{bal}_1, \rho, \rho', s, s', \mathrm{Path}$.

Above are the high level instance and witness values known before the protocol starts and don't include instance and witness created during protocol execution like the various blindings and $T$ values.

#### Prover

1. Prover wants to prove knowledge of $\mathrm{bal}_1, \rho, s$ in the relation $\mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot G_1 + \rho \cdot G_4 + s \cdot G_6 = \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r} + v \cdot G_1 - \mathrm{AK} - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3$.
2. Similarly, prover wants to prove knowledge of $\mathrm{bal}_1, \rho', s'$ in $\mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot G_1 + \rho' \cdot G_4 + s' \cdot G_6 = \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}} - \mathrm{AK} - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3$ where the RHS is public.
3. Prover gets the path of the leaf $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}}$ as $\mathrm{Path}$, an array of nodes (curve points), and randomizes it by adding blindings to all nodes. This will result in the leaf $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}}$ being transformed into $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r} = \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}} + b_0 \cdot H_0$ and $\mathrm{Path}$ transforms into $\mathrm{Path}_r$. Prover enforces the constraints for curve tree membership using $\mathrm{Path}, \mathrm{Path}_r, b_i$.
4. Since prover knows the opening of $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}}$ and $b_0$, it can prove the knowledge of opening of $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}$ as well. So relation 1 can be transformed as:
   $$
 \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot G_1 + \rho \cdot G_4 + s \cdot G_6 + b_0 \cdot H_0 = \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r} + v \cdot G_1 - \mathrm{AK} - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3
 $$
   where the RHS is public.
5. Prover picks random
   
   $$\mathrm{sk}_r, \mathrm{bal}_{1_r}, \rho_r, \rho'_r, s_r, s'_r, b_{0_r} \stackrel{\$}{\leftarrow} \mathbb{Z}_p $$
   
   and creates:
   $$
 T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}} = \mathrm{bal}_{1_r} \cdot G_1 + \rho_r \cdot G_4 + s_r \cdot G_6 + b_{0_r} \cdot H_0, \in \mathbb{G}_p
 $$
6. For the new state, create
   $$
 T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}} = \mathrm{bal}_{1_r} \cdot G_1 + \rho'_r \cdot G_4 + s'_r \cdot G_6, \in \mathbb{G}_p
 $$
   Same blinding $\mathrm{bal}_{1_r}$ is used for both old and new state as the equation of $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}$ has been adjusted to keep balance witness same.
7. For the correctness of nullifier and knowledge of secret key, prover creates:
   $$T_{\mathrm{null}} = \rho_r \cdot G_4, \in \mathbb{G}_p$$
   $$T_{\mathrm{pk}} = \mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}, \in \mathbb{G}_p$$
8. For enforcing $\mathrm{bal}_1 \leq \mathrm{MAX\_FEE\_BALANCE}$, prover sets up constraints in Bulletproof. Let $C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}}$ be the commitment:
   $$
 C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}} = b' \cdot H_0 + \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot H_1, \in \mathbb{G}_p
 $$

9. Prover creates commitment for $C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}}$ by picking random $r_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}} \in \mathbb{Z}_p$:
   $$
 T_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}} = r_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}} \cdot H_0 + \mathrm{bal}_{1_r} \cdot H_1, \in \mathbb{G}_p
 $$

10. Prover hashes the following to create challenge $c$ as:
    $$c = \mathrm{Hash}(\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}, \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}, \mathrm{AK}, \mathrm{at}, N, v, \mathrm{Path}_r, \mathrm{Root}, C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}}, T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}, T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}}, T_{\mathrm{null}}, T_{\mathrm{pk}}, T_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}})
 $$
11. Prover creates responses for $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}$:
    $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}} = [\begin{matrix}
 \mathrm{bal}_{1_r} + \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot c, & \rho_r + \rho \cdot c, & s_r + s \cdot c, & b_{0_r} + b_0 \cdot c\\
 \end{matrix}]$$

12. Prover creates responses for $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{new}}}$:
    $$
 \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{new}}} = [\begin{matrix}
 \bot, & \rho'_r + \rho' \cdot c, & s'_r + s' \cdot c\\
 \end{matrix}]
 $$
    The symbol $\bot$ indicates that no response is created for $\mathrm{bal}_1$ since a response has been created for the same witness in $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}$.

13. Response for nullifier:
    $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{null}} = \rho_r + \rho \cdot c$

14. Response for secret key:
    $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{pk}} = \mathrm{sk}_r + \mathrm{sk} \cdot c$

15. For relation $C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}}$, only the response for blinding $b'$ needs to be created:
    $\mathrm{resp}_{b'} = r_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}} + b' \cdot c$

16. The proof is
    $$
 (C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}}, \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}, \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}, \mathrm{Path}_r, N, $$
    $$T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}, T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}}, T_{\mathrm{null}}, T_{\mathrm{pk}}, T_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}}, \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}, \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{new}}}, \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{null}}, \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{pk}}, \mathrm{resp}_{b'})
 $$

#### Verifier

1. Verifier hashes the following to create challenge $c$ as:
   $$
 c = \mathrm{Hash}(\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}, \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}, \mathrm{AK}, \mathrm{at}, N, v, \mathrm{Path}_r, \mathrm{Root}, C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}}, T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}, T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}}, T_{\mathrm{null}}, T_{\mathrm{pk}}, T_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}})
 $$
2. Verifier enforces the Bulletproof constraint for the balance change: $\mathrm{bal}_1 \leq \mathrm{MAX\_FEE\_BALANCE}$. (Constraints in the appendix)

3. Verifier enforces the Bulletproof constraints for curve tree membership to verify that $\mathrm{Path}_r$ leads to $\mathrm{Root}$. (Constraints in the appendix)

4. Verifies correctness of $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}$ by checking:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[0] \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[1] \cdot G_4 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[2] \cdot G_6 + $$
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[3] \cdot H_0 \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}} + (\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r} + v \cdot G_1 - \mathrm{AK} - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3) \cdot c$$

   $$\implies (\mathrm{bal}_{1_r} + \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot c) \cdot G_1 + (\rho_r + \rho \cdot c) \cdot G_4 + (s_r + s \cdot c) \cdot G_6 + $$
   $$(b_{0_r} + b_0 \cdot c) \cdot H_0 \stackrel{?}{=} \mathrm{bal}_{1_r} \cdot G_1 + \rho_r \cdot G_4 + s_r \cdot G_6 + b_{0_r} \cdot H_0 + (\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r} + v \cdot G_1 - \mathrm{AK} - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3) \cdot c$$

5. Verifies correctness of $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}$ by checking:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[0] \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{new}}}[1] \cdot G_4 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{new}}}[2] \cdot G_6 \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}} + (\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}} - \mathrm{AK} - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3) \cdot c$$

   $$\implies (\mathrm{bal}_{1_r} + \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot c) \cdot G_1 + (\rho'_r + \rho' \cdot c) \cdot G_4 + (s'_r + s' \cdot c) \cdot G_6 \stackrel{?}{=} \mathrm{bal}_{1_r} \cdot G_1 + \rho'_r \cdot G_4 + s'_r \cdot G_6 + (\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}} - \mathrm{AK} - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3) \cdot c$$

   Note that for the $\mathrm{bal}_1$ component, verifier reuses the response from $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}$ as indicated by $\bot$ in $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{new}}}$.

6. Verifies correctness of nullifier by checking:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{null}} \cdot G_4 \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{null}} + N \cdot c$$
   $$\implies (\rho_r + \rho \cdot c) \cdot G_4 \stackrel{?}{=} \rho_r \cdot G_4 + N \cdot c$$

7. Verifies knowledge of secret key by checking:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{pk}} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{pk}} + \mathrm{AK} \cdot c$$
   $$\implies (\mathrm{sk}_r + \mathrm{sk} \cdot c) \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} \stackrel{?}{=} \mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{AK} \cdot c$$

8. Verifier checks response for $C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}}$ as:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{b'} \cdot H_0 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[0] \cdot H_1 \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}} + C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}} \cdot c$$

   $$\implies (r_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}} + b' \cdot c) \cdot H_0 + (\mathrm{bal}_{1_r} + \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot c) \cdot H_1 \stackrel{?}{=} r_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}} \cdot H_0 + \mathrm{bal}_{1_r} \cdot H_1 + C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}} \cdot c$$

9. Verifier finally checks the Bulletproofs proof for all constraints i.e. curve tree membership and balance change


### Split prover variant (W2/W3)

In W2/W3 the work is split between a host (which does not know $\mathrm{sk}$) and a device (which holds $\mathrm{sk}$). Since $\mathrm{AK}$ is revealed during top-up, the host's two statements drop the $\mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}$ component and the verifier subtracts $\mathrm{AK}$ out. Concretely:
$$
\mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot G_1 + \rho \cdot G_4 + s \cdot G_6 + b_0 \cdot H_0 = \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r} + v \cdot G_1 - \mathrm{AK} - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3
$$
$$
\mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot G_1 + \rho' \cdot G_4 + s' \cdot G_6 = \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}} - \mathrm{AK} - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3
$$
Everything else the host does (nullifier $N = \rho \cdot G_4$, curve tree membership for $\mathrm{Path}_r$, the $C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}}$ commitment and its range proof constraint, sharing $\mathrm{bal}_{1_r}$ between $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}$ and $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}$) is unchanged. The proof of knowledge of $\mathrm{sk}$ in $\mathrm{AK}$ and the $T_{\mathrm{pk}}$ term are dropped from the host's side.

The device produces a standalone Sigma proof of knowledge of $\mathrm{sk}$ in $\mathrm{AK} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}$.

**Instance**: $\mathrm{AK}, G_{\mathrm{Aff}}$

**Witness**: $\mathrm{sk}$

#### Device prover
1. Pick random $\mathrm{sk}_r \in \mathbb{Z}_p$ and compute
   $$T_{\mathrm{AK}} = \mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}$$
2. Hash the device's transcript to get a challenge
   $$c = \mathrm{Hash}(\mathrm{nonce}, \mathrm{AK}, T_{\mathrm{AK}})$$
3. Response
   $$s_{\mathrm{sk}} = \mathrm{sk}_r + \mathrm{sk} \cdot c$$

The device sends $(T_{\mathrm{AK}}, s_{\mathrm{sk}})$ to the verifier (or, in W3, to the host first).

   $$c = \mathrm{Hash}(\mathrm{nonce}, \mathrm{AK}, T_{\mathrm{AK}})$$
2. Verify the affirmation key relation:
   $$s_{\mathrm{sk}} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{AK}} + \mathrm{AK} \cdot c$$

It then derives its challenge from that transcript exactly as the W1 challenge $c$ is derived, but with $T_{\mathrm{pk}}$ omitted from the hash; call this challenge $c_H$. The host sends $c_H$ to the device. The device sets its $\mathrm{nonce}$ to
$$\mathrm{nonce}' = c_H \| \mathrm{nonce}$$
(concatenation of the byte serialization of $c_H$ and the original $\mathrm{nonce}$), runs the Device prover above against $\mathrm{nonce}'$ — so its challenge becomes
$$c = \mathrm{Hash}(\mathrm{nonce}', \mathrm{AK}, T_{\mathrm{AK}}) = \mathrm{Hash}((c_H \| \mathrm{nonce}), \mathrm{AK}, T_{\mathrm{AK}})$$
— and sends $(T_{\mathrm{AK}}, s_{\mathrm{sk}})$ back to the host. The host appends the device proof bytes to its transcript and derives a fresh challenge
$$c_H' = \mathrm{Hash}(c_H, T_{\mathrm{AK}}, s_{\mathrm{sk}})$$
which it uses for its own response generation. The verifier mirrors the same ordering: derives $c_H$, reconstructs $\mathrm{nonce}' = c_H \| \mathrm{nonce}$, verifies the device proof against $\mathrm{nonce}'$, then derives $c_H'$ from the device proof bytes and verifies the host's responses against $c_H'$.


## Fee payment
To pay of amount $v$, the old account state is transitioned to a new one whose balance is $v$ less than the previous one. The user must always reveal the asset-id for fee.

A successful completion of fee payment results in the following:

1. The user's old account state $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}}$ is invalidated and new state $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}$ is created. However, $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}}$ cannot be revealed as it identifies which account state is being invalidated. So a randomized version of it $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}$ is used in relations.
2. Nullifier $N=\rho \cdot G_4$ is revealed by issuer and chain adds it to the nullifier set. Here $\rho$ refers to the nullifier secret key of $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}}$
3. The balance in $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}$ is less than balance in $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}}$ by the fee amount
4. $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}$ is inserted as a new leaf in the curve tree.

### Protocol

_The following description is for W1 (solo) where a single prover knows the secret key $\mathrm{sk}$ along with all other witnesses. The split-prover variant used in W2/W3 is described after the Verifier section below._

Here the prover (user) wants to prove following relations when minting amount $v$:

1. $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{bal}_0 \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + s \cdot G_6$. Since $\mathrm{bal}_0 - v = \mathrm{bal}_1$, this is equivalent to $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}} + v \cdot G_1 = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + s \cdot G_6$
2. Similarly, $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho' \cdot G_4 + s' \cdot G_6$.
3. $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}}$ exists in the accumulator (account curve tree)
4. $N = \rho \cdot G_4$
5. $\mathrm{bal}_1 = \mathrm{bal}_0 - v$
6. $\mathrm{bal}_1 \leq \mathrm{MAX\_FEE\_BALANCE}$

**Instance**: $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}, \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}, \mathrm{at}, N, v, \mathrm{Path}_r, \mathrm{Root}, G_{\mathrm{Aff}}, G_i, H_i$

**Witness**: $\mathrm{sk}, \mathrm{bal}_0, \mathrm{bal}_1, \rho, \rho', s, s', \mathrm{Path}$.

Above are the high level instance and witness values known before the protocol starts and don't include instance and witness created during protocol execution like the various blindings and $T$ values.

#### Prover

1. Prover wants to prove knowledge of $\mathrm{sk}, \mathrm{bal}_1, \rho, s$ in the relation $\mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot G_1 + \rho \cdot G_4 + s \cdot G_6 = \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r} + v \cdot G_1 - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 - \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}$.
2. Similarly, prover wants to prove knowledge of $\mathrm{sk}, \mathrm{bal}_1, \rho', s'$ in $\mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot G_1 + \rho' \cdot G_4 + s' \cdot G_6 = \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}} - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 - \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}$ where the RHS is public.
3. Prover gets the path of the leaf $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}}$ as $\mathrm{Path}$, an array of nodes (curve points), and randomizes it by adding blindings to all nodes. This will result in the leaf $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}}$ being transformed into $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r} = \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}} + b_0 \cdot H_0$ and $\mathrm{Path}$ transforms into $\mathrm{Path}_r$. Prover enforces the constraints for curve tree membership using $\mathrm{Path}, \mathrm{Path}_r, b_i$.
4. Since prover knows the opening of $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}}$ and $b_0$, it can prove the knowledge of opening of $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}$ as well. So relation 1 can be transformed as:
   $$
 \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot G_1 + \rho \cdot G_4 + s \cdot G_6 + b_0 \cdot H_0 = \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r} + v \cdot G_1 - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3
 $$
   where the RHS is public.
5. Prover picks random:
   
   $$\mathrm{sk}_r, \mathrm{bal}_{1_r}, \rho_r, \rho'_r, s_r, s'_r, b_{0_r} \stackrel{\$}{\leftarrow} \mathbb{Z}_p$$
   
   and creates:
   $$
 T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}} = \mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{bal}_{1_r} \cdot G_1 + \rho_r \cdot G_4 + s_r \cdot G_6 + b_{0_r} \cdot H_0, \in \mathbb{G}_p
 $$
6. For the new state, create:
   $$
 T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}} = \mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{bal}_{1_r} \cdot G_1 + \rho'_r \cdot G_4 + s'_r \cdot G_6, \in \mathbb{G}_p
 $$

   Same blindings for $\mathrm{sk}, \mathrm{bal}_1$ are used in both old and new state since these values don't change.
7. For the correctness of nullifier, prover creates:
   $$T_{\mathrm{null}} = \rho_r \cdot G_4, \in \mathbb{G}_p$$
8. For enforcing $\mathrm{bal}_1 \leq \mathrm{MAX\_FEE\_BALANCE}$, prover sets up constraints in Bulletproof. Let $C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}}$ be the commitment:
   $$C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}} = b' \cdot H_0 + \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot H_1, \in \mathbb{G}_p$$
9. Prover creates commitment for $C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}}$ by picking random $r_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}} \in \mathbb{Z}_p$:
   $$T_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}} = r_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}} \cdot H_0 + \mathrm{bal}_{1_r} \cdot H_1, \in \mathbb{G}_p$$
10. Prover hashes the following to create challenge $c$ as:
    $$c = \mathrm{Hash}(\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}, \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}, \mathrm{at}, N, v, \mathrm{Path}_r, \mathrm{Root}, C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}}, T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}, T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}}, T_{\mathrm{null}}, T_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}})
 $$
11. Prover creates responses for $\mathrm{Resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}$:
    $$\mathrm{Resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}} = [\begin{matrix}
 \mathrm{sk}_r + \mathrm{sk} \cdot c, & \mathrm{bal}_{1_r} + \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot c, & \rho_r + \rho \cdot c, & s_r + s \cdot c, & b_{0_r} + b_0 \cdot c\\
 \end{matrix}]$$

12. Prover creates responses for $\mathrm{Resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{new}}}$:
    $$\mathrm{Resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{new}}} = [\begin{matrix}
 \bot, & \bot, & \rho'_r + \rho' \cdot c, & s'_r + s' \cdot c\\
 \end{matrix}]$$
    The symbol $\bot$ indicates that no response is created for $\mathrm{sk}, \mathrm{bal}_1$ since responses have been created for the same witnesses in $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}$.

13. Response for nullifier:
    $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{null}} = \rho_r + \rho \cdot c$

14. For relation $C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}}$, only the response for blinding $b'$ needs to be created:
    $\mathrm{resp}_{b'} = r_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}} + b' \cdot c$

15. The proof is
    $$
 (C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}}, \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}, \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}, \mathrm{Path}_r, N, $$
    $$T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}, T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}}, T_{\mathrm{null}}, T_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}}, \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}, \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{new}}}, \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{null}}, \mathrm{resp}_{b'})
 $$

#### Verifier

1. Verifier hashes the following to create challenge $c$ as:
   $$
 c = \mathrm{Hash}(\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}, \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}, \mathrm{at}, N, v, \mathrm{Path}_r, \mathrm{Root}, C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}}, T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}, T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}}, T_{\mathrm{null}}, T_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}})
 $$
2. Verifier enforces the Bulletproof constraint for the balance range: $\mathrm{bal}_1 \leq \mathrm{MAX\_FEE\_BALANCE}$. (Constraints in the appendix)
3. Verifier enforces the Bulletproof constraints for curve tree membership to verify that $\mathrm{Path}_r$ leads to $\mathrm{Root}$. (Constraints in the appendix)
4. Verifies correctness of $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}$ by checking:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[0] \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[1] \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[2] \cdot G_4 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[3] \cdot G_6 + $$
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[4] \cdot H_0 \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}} + (\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r} + v \cdot G_1 - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3) \cdot c$$

   $$\implies (\mathrm{sk}_r + \mathrm{sk} \cdot c) \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + (\mathrm{bal}_{1_r} + \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot c) \cdot G_1 + (\rho_r + \rho \cdot c) \cdot G_4 + (s_r + s \cdot c) \cdot G_6 + $$
   $$(b_{0_r} + b_0 \cdot c) \cdot H_0 \stackrel{?}{=} \mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{bal}_{1_r} \cdot G_1 + \rho_r \cdot G_4 + s_r \cdot G_6 + b_{0_r} \cdot H_0 + (\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r} + v \cdot G_1 - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3) \cdot c$$

5. Verifies correctness of $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}$ by checking:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[0] \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[1] \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{new}}}[2] \cdot G_4 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{new}}}[3] \cdot G_6 \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}} + (\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}} - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3) \cdot c$$

   $$\implies (\mathrm{sk}_r + \mathrm{sk} \cdot c) \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + (\mathrm{bal}_{1_r} + \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot c) \cdot G_1 + (\rho'_r + \rho' \cdot c) \cdot G_4 + $$
   $$(s'_r + s' \cdot c) \cdot G_6 \stackrel{?}{=} \mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + \mathrm{bal}_{1_r} \cdot G_1 + \rho'_r \cdot G_4 + s'_r \cdot G_6 + (\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}} - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3) \cdot c$$
   Note that for the $\mathrm{sk}, \mathrm{bal}_1$ components, verifier reuses responses from $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}$ as indicated by $\bot$ in $\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{new}}}$.
6. Verifies correctness of nullifier by checking:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[2] \cdot G_4 \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{null}} + N \cdot c$$
   $$\implies (\rho_r + \rho \cdot c) \cdot G_4 \stackrel{?}{=} \rho_r \cdot G_4 + N \cdot c$$
7. Verifier checks response for $C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}}$ as:
   $$\mathrm{resp}_{b'} \cdot H_0 + \mathrm{resp}_{\mathrm{state}_{\mathrm{old}_r}}[1] \cdot H_1 \stackrel{?}{=} T_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}} + C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}} \cdot c$$
   $$\implies (r_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}} + b' \cdot c) \cdot H_0 + (\mathrm{bal}_{1_r} + \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot c) \cdot H_1 \stackrel{?}{=} r_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}} \cdot H_0 + \mathrm{bal}_{1_r} \cdot H_1 + C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}} \cdot c$$
8. Verifier finally checks the Bulletproofs proof for all constraints i.e. curve tree membership and balance range


### Split prover variant (W2/W3)

Unlike fee account registration and top-up, here $\mathrm{AK}$ is not revealed — the $\mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}$ term lives inside the account commitment. So neither party alone can prove knowledge of the opening of the actual commitment. Instead the device and the host each produce a *partial commitment* and the verifier checks that the two partials add up to the real commitment.

Both the new state's commitment randomness $s'$ and the rerandomization blinding $b_0$ of the old leaf are split additively between the two parties:
$$
s' = s'_D + s'_H, \quad b_0 = b_{0_D} + b_{0_H}
$$
where the device picks $s'_D, b_{0_D}$ and the host picks $s'_H, b_{0_H}$. Each party then forms its partial commitment.

For the new state commitment $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}$:
$$
P_{\mathrm{new},D} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + s'_D \cdot G_6
$$
$$
P_{\mathrm{new},H} = \mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho' \cdot G_4 + s'_H \cdot G_6
$$
By construction $P_{\mathrm{new},D} + P_{\mathrm{new},H} = \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}$.

For the re-randomized old state $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r} = \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}} + b_0 \cdot H_0$:
$$
P_{\mathrm{old},D} = \mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + b_{0_D} \cdot H_0
$$
$$
P_{\mathrm{old},H} = \mathrm{bal}_0 \cdot G_1 + \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3 + \rho \cdot G_4 + s \cdot G_6 + b_{0_H} \cdot H_0
$$
By construction $P_{\mathrm{old},D} + P_{\mathrm{old},H} = \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}$.

The device publishes $P_{\mathrm{old},D}$ and $P_{\mathrm{new},D}$. The host runs the same protocol as above except its two statements are over $P_{\mathrm{old},H}$ and $P_{\mathrm{new},H}$ (in place of $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}$ and $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}$), with the $\mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}$ term dropped from both:
$$
\mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot G_1 + \rho \cdot G_4 + s \cdot G_6 + b_{0_H} \cdot H_0 = P_{\mathrm{old},H} + v \cdot G_1 - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3
$$
$$
\mathrm{bal}_1 \cdot G_1 + \rho' \cdot G_4 + s'_H \cdot G_6 = P_{\mathrm{new},H} - \mathrm{at} \cdot G_3
$$
The proof of knowledge of $\mathrm{sk}$ is dropped from the host's side; nullifier, curve tree membership for $\mathrm{Path}_r$, and $C_{\mathrm{BP}_{\mathrm{bal}}}$ with its range constraint are unchanged.

The verifier additionally checks
$$P_{\mathrm{old},D} + P_{\mathrm{old},H} \stackrel{?}{=} \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}, \quad P_{\mathrm{new},D} + P_{\mathrm{new},H} \stackrel{?}{=} \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}$$
before accepting.

The device produces a Sigma proof of knowledge of $\mathrm{sk}, b_{0_D}$ in $P_{\mathrm{old},D}$ and $\mathrm{sk}, s'_D$ in $P_{\mathrm{new},D}$, with the same $\mathrm{sk}$ shared between the two relations.

**Instance**: $P_{\mathrm{old},D}, P_{\mathrm{new},D}, G_{\mathrm{Aff}}, G_6, H_0$

**Witness**: $\mathrm{sk}, b_{0_D}, s'_D$

#### Device prover
1. Pick random $\mathrm{sk}_r, b_{0_{D_r}}, s'_{D_r} \in \mathbb{Z}_p$ and compute
   $$T_{P_{\mathrm{old},D}} = \mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + b_{0_{D_r}} \cdot H_0$$
   $$T_{P_{\mathrm{new},D}} = \mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + s'_{D_r} \cdot G_6$$
2. Hash the device's transcript to get a challenge
   $$c = \mathrm{Hash}(\mathrm{nonce}, N, \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}, \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}, P_{\mathrm{old},D}, P_{\mathrm{new},D}, T_{P_{\mathrm{old},D}}, T_{P_{\mathrm{new},D}})$$
3. Responses
   $$s_{\mathrm{sk}} = \mathrm{sk}_r + \mathrm{sk} \cdot c, \quad s_{b_{0_D}} = b_{0_{D_r}} + b_{0_D} \cdot c, \quad s_{s'_D} = s'_{D_r} + s'_D \cdot c$$

The device sends $(T_{P_{\mathrm{old},D}}, T_{P_{\mathrm{new},D}}, P_{\mathrm{old},D}, P_{\mathrm{new},D}, s_{\mathrm{sk}}, s_{b_{0_D}}, s_{s'_D})$ to the verifier (or, in W3, to the host first).

#### Device verifier
1. Recompute the challenge:
   $$c = \mathrm{Hash}(\mathrm{nonce}, N, \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}, \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}, P_{\mathrm{old},D}, P_{\mathrm{new},D}, T_{P_{\mathrm{old},D}}, T_{P_{\mathrm{new},D}})$$
2. Verify the old partial:
   $$s_{\mathrm{sk}} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + s_{b_{0_D}} \cdot H_0 \stackrel{?}{=} T_{P_{\mathrm{old},D}} + P_{\mathrm{old},D} \cdot c$$
   $$\implies (\mathrm{sk}_r + \mathrm{sk} \cdot c) \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + (b_{0_{D_r}} + b_{0_D} \cdot c) \cdot H_0 \stackrel{?}{=} \mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + b_{0_{D_r}} \cdot H_0 + P_{\mathrm{old},D} \cdot c$$
3. Verify the new partial:
   $$s_{\mathrm{sk}} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + s_{s'_D} \cdot G_6 \stackrel{?}{=} T_{P_{\mathrm{new},D}} + P_{\mathrm{new},D} \cdot c$$
   $$\implies (\mathrm{sk}_r + \mathrm{sk} \cdot c) \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + (s'_{D_r} + s'_D \cdot c) \cdot G_6 \stackrel{?}{=} \mathrm{sk}_r \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}} + s'_{D_r} \cdot G_6 + P_{\mathrm{new},D} \cdot c$$

Reusing $s_{\mathrm{sk}}$ in both checks binds the same $\mathrm{sk}$ across the two partials.

#### Binding host and device

**W2 (parallel).** The device's $\mathrm{nonce}$ is the same $\mathrm{nonce}$ the host uses, so both proofs end up bound to the same context. The two proofs are produced independently. At verification time, in addition to the host and device sigma checks, the verifier also performs the partial-commitment sum checks above.

**W3 (sequential).** The host first runs its pre-challenge phase of the Prover above — picking all blindings and computing all $T$ values on the host side, but using $P_{\mathrm{old},H}, P_{\mathrm{new},H}$ in place of $\mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}, \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}$ and dropping the $\mathrm{sk} \cdot G_{\mathrm{Aff}}$ term (so $T_{\mathrm{pk}}$, if any, is not computed and not added to its transcript). It then derives its challenge from that transcript exactly as the W1 challenge $c$ is derived but adapted to the host's statements; call this challenge $c_H$. The host sends $c_H$ to the device. The device sets its $\mathrm{nonce}$ to
$$\mathrm{nonce}' = c_H \| \mathrm{nonce}$$
(concatenation of the byte serialization of $c_H$ and the original $\mathrm{nonce}$), runs the Device prover above against $\mathrm{nonce}'$ — so its challenge becomes
$$c = \mathrm{Hash}(\mathrm{nonce}', N, \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}, \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}, P_{\mathrm{old},D}, P_{\mathrm{new},D}, T_{P_{\mathrm{old},D}}, T_{P_{\mathrm{new},D}}) = \mathrm{Hash}((c_H \| \mathrm{nonce}), N, \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{old}_r}, \mathrm{State}_{\mathrm{new}}, P_{\mathrm{old},D}, P_{\mathrm{new},D}, T_{P_{\mathrm{old},D}}, T_{P_{\mathrm{new},D}})$$
— and sends $(T_{P_{\mathrm{old},D}}, T_{P_{\mathrm{new},D}}, P_{\mathrm{old},D}, P_{\mathrm{new},D}, s_{\mathrm{sk}}, s_{b_{0_D}}, s_{s'_D})$ back to the host. The host appends the device proof bytes to its transcript and derives a fresh challenge
$$c_H' = \mathrm{Hash}(c_H, T_{P_{\mathrm{old},D}}, T_{P_{\mathrm{new},D}}, P_{\mathrm{old},D}, P_{\mathrm{new},D}, s_{\mathrm{sk}}, s_{b_{0_D}}, s_{s'_D})$$
which it uses for its own response generation. The verifier mirrors the same ordering: derives $c_H$, reconstructs $\mathrm{nonce}' = c_H \| \mathrm{nonce}$, verifies the device proof against $\mathrm{nonce}'$, then derives $c_H'$ from the device proof bytes and verifies the host's responses against $c_H'$.
